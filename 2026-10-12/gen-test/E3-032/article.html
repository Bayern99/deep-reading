房屋建造商与卖家争相争夺买家Home Builders and Sellers Vie for Buyers

竞争日益激烈，这将迫使莱纳和KB Home这样的公司加大激励措施力度。Competition is heating up, which will force companies like Lennar and KB Home to beef up incentives

如今抵押贷款利率已突破7%，你准备好正视自己房产的实际价值了吗？建筑商们可不会因此感谢你。Ready to be realistic about the value of your home now that mortgage rates are above 7%? Builders won't thank you for it.

过去四年里，房屋建筑商有一个有利条件：普通房主不愿降低售价，因此存量房市场的竞争相对较弱。如今这种情况正在发生变化，这将进一步挤压建筑商本就微薄的利润空间。Home builders had one thing working in their favor over the past four years: Regular home sellers weren't willing to budge on prices, so competition from the existing home market was low. This is starting to change, which will hurt builders' already diminished profit margins.

莱纳公司和KB Home最近表示，他们正面临来自那些出售意愿更强的现有房主的激烈竞争。在截至8月的三个月里，莱纳公司在西部地区的房屋平均售价比去年同期下降了44,000美元，降幅达7%。其中部地区的平均房价则同比下降了5%。该公司指出，存量房市场的激烈竞争是导致这一降幅的原因之一。Lennar and KB Home said recently that they are facing tougher competition from regular owners who are more motivated to sell. The average price Lennar sold homes for in its west region over the three months through August was $44,000 lower than the same period last year—a 7% drop. Average prices in its central region dropped 5% compared with a year ago. It said stiffer competition from the existing-home market was one factor behind the declines.

KB Home将第四季度的平均售价预期中值从50万美元下调至48万美元。南加州的房屋卖家纷纷降价以吸引买家，因此KB Home也不得不跟进降价，否则可能错失销售机会。KB Home lowered the midpoint of its average sales-price estimate for the fourth quarter from $500,000 to$480,000. Southern Californian home sellers are cutting their prices to entice buyers, so KB Home has to follow or risk missing out on sales.

Redfin的数据显示，在截至9月20日的四周内，现有住房市场中有21%的卖家降低了挂牌价。目前最活跃的现象是各种购房优惠措施，而这些优惠并未反映在房价数据中。In the four weeks through Sept. 20, 21% of sellers in the existing-home market dropped their asking price, data from Redfin shows. The most significant activity is happening with concessions, which don't show up in headline prices.

8月份，45%的售房者向买家提供了优惠措施，比如承担交易手续费或提供资金用于房屋修缮。在亚特兰大和田纳西州纳什维尔等房源供过于求的阳光带地区，如今有七成售房者都提供了此类优惠。这些也正是房屋建筑商们倾向于集中布局的市场。In August, 45% of home sellers gave concessions to buyers, such as offering to cover the closing costs or handing the buyer money for repairs. In oversupplied Sunbelt markets such as Atlanta and Nashville, Tenn., 7 in 10 sellers are now offering concessions. These are the same markets where home builders tend to cluster.

直接降价的情况可能会愈发普遍，因为待售房屋数量已几乎恢复至疫情前水平。根据Realtor.com的数据，9月份的房源数量仅比2019年同期少了5%。Outright price cuts are likely to become more common because the number of homes for sale is almost back to pre-pandemic levels. In September, inventory was 5% below the same month of 2019, according to data from Realtor.com.

疫情期间的房地产繁荣彻底打乱了新房市场与存量房市场之间原有的竞争格局。去年，存量房销量比2022年水平下降了五分之一。The pandemic-era housing boom sent the usual competitive dynamic between the new-home and existing-home markets haywire. Last year, sales of existing homes were one-fifth below 2022 levels.

四年前，当抵押贷款利率开始上升时，那些享受着极低利率的房主们选择继续持房不动，导致待售房源严重短缺。许多卖房者则抱着“这就是我的要价，能接受就买吧”的心态。他们宁愿将房产撤出市场，也不愿接受较低的报价。Once mortgage rates began to rise four years ago, owners with ultralow rates stayed put, starving the for-sale market of supply. And many sellers had a "here is my price, make me move" mentality. They preferred to take their property off the market rather than accept a lower offer.

这可正合建筑商的心意。That suited builders nicely.

若想在市场上日益增多的库存中保持竞争力，建筑商们就不得不投入资金。Builders will have to cough up money if they want to stay competitive with the swelling inventory on the market.

他们介入以填补现有住房库存的缺口。2022年至2025年间，新房销量增长了7%。They stepped in to fill the gap in existing-home inventory. Sales of new homes rose 7% between 2022 and 2025.

大型建筑商得以持续售房，是因为他们能够提供抵押贷款利率补贴。过去几年里，任何从建筑商处购买新房的人都能获得比从传统贷款机构申请时低1至2个百分点的贷款利率。Large builders were able to keep selling homes because they could sell off a mortgage-rate buy-down. Over the past few years, anyone buying a new home from a builder has been able to get a loan that is 1 to 2 percentage points lower than what she or he would get from a primary lender.

不利的一面是，这些利率补贴措施成本高昂，严重挤压了利润空间。目前，莱纳公司不得不提供相当于房屋价值12%的利率补贴等激励措施才能促成交易。在截至8月的三个月里，该公司的毛利率为15.8%——这一水平与2008-09年金融危机后的情况相当。The downside is that the buy-downs are expensive and have sapped profit margins. Lennar currently has to offer incentives such as rate buydowns worth 12% of the value of a home to get deals done. In the three months through August, the company's gross margin was 15.8%—back at levels last seen in the aftermath of the 2008-09 financial crisis.

情况可能会变得更糟。更高的抵押贷款利率使得建筑商更难从贷款机构获得预先承诺——即以低于市场水平的价格批量购买抵押贷款的协议。Things might get worse. Higher mortgage rates make it costlier for builders to buy forward commitments from lenders, which is an agreement to buy mortgages in bulk at a below-market rate.

据美国企业研究所住房中心主任爱德华·平托介绍，去年年底，一家建筑商不得不支付380万美元，才能将一笔1亿美元远期抵押贷款融资协议的利率从6%降至5%。At the end of last year, a builder had to pay $3.8 million to buy down the rate from 6% to 5% on a$100 million forward mortgage funding commitment, according to Edward Pinto, director of the AEI Housing Center.

如今，抵押贷款利率的上升使得为将利率降低1个百分点所需的成本增加——对于同样1亿美元的远期承诺而言，这一成本已升至540万美元。若想在市场上日益增多的房源中保持竞争力，建筑商们就不得不掏出这笔钱。Today's higher mortgage rates have pushed the cost of buying down the rate by 1 percentage point on that same $100 million forward commitment to$5.4 million. Builders will have to cough up the money if they want to stay competitive with the swelling inventory on the market.

建筑商与普通房产卖家也在争夺日益减少的买家群体。自抵押贷款利率升至7%以上后，市场需求急剧下滑。通常情况下，随着购房季接近尾声，9月份每个社区的新房销量会比8月份平均下降4%。然而据约翰·伯恩斯研究与咨询公司的研究总监里克·帕拉西奥斯称，今年这一降幅达到了18%。Builders and regular sellers also are fighting over a shrinking pool of buyers. Demand has nosedived since mortgage rates rose above 7%. Sales of new homes per community in September normally slow by 4% on average compared with August as the buying season winds down. This year, sales fell 18%, according to Rick Palacios, director of research at John Burns Research and Consulting.

今年，伯克希尔·哈撒韦一直在逆势押注房地产建筑商。5月份，该公司斥资68亿美元收购了泰勒·莫里森。9月份，其持有的莱纳公司股份比例又提升至10%以上。但这并非建筑行业即将复苏的迹象。伯克希尔是在一个当前形势艰难的市场中大胆出手，并坚信未来几年情况会有所好转。Berkshire Hathaway has been making contrarian bets on builders this year. It paid $6.8 billion in May to buy Taylor Morrison. In September, it took its stake in Lennar above 10%. This isn't a sign of an imminent recovery for builders. Berkshire is buying in a tough neighborhood with confidence it will improve over the next few years.

美国仍然存在住房短缺现象。瑞银集团的住房建筑行业分析师约翰·洛瓦洛指出，住房市场的严峻环境正迫使建筑商提升运营效率。不少建筑商已将施工时间缩短至历史最低水平。There is still a shortage of housing in America. And UBS home-builders analyst John Lovallo points out that harsh conditions in the housing market are forcing builders to become better operators. Several whittled their construction times down to record lows.

这是一场长期赌博。目前，建筑商的前景不容乐观。抵押贷款利率正朝着不利的方向发展。他们最不希望看到的就是与普通房屋卖家爆发价格战。但看来这场价格战即将来临。That's a long-term bet. For now, the outlook for builders is tough. Mortgage rates are moving in the wrong direction. The last thing they want is a price war with regular home sellers. But it looks like there is one on the way.
