马萨诸塞州的政策导致电费上涨Massachusetts’ Policies Increase Electric Bills

回复：“气候现实冲击贝肯山”（10月8日）Re: “Climate Reality Hits Beacon Hill” (Oct. 8)

Daniel Turner, Executive director and founder, Power The Future, Round Hill, Va. 2026年10月12日 · 《华尔街日报》A16 版 Oct 12, 2026 · The Wall Street Journal, p.A16

关于您发表于10月8日的社论《气候现实冲击贝肯山》：美国东北部地区主要由一些“蓝色州”的政府掌管，那里的政客们既受制于不切实际的“绿色”议程，又迫切希望谈论“可负担性”——这两者根本无法兼得。Regarding your editorial "Climate Reality Hits Beacon Hill" (Oct. 8): The Northeast is dominated by blue-state governments where politicians are beholden to an unrealistic "green" agenda and are simultaneously desperate to talk about "affordability"—an incompatible combination.

反对本土且可靠的能源供应的做法导致了管道项目的受阻。这进而阻碍了从邻近的宾夕法尼亚州等地获取天然气——该州是美国天然气产量第二高的州。天然气为美国超过40%的电力供应提供动力，而美国也是全球最大的天然气生产国。这本应是一件显而易见、无需多虑的事情。Opposition to domestic and reliable energy leads to the obstruction of pipeline projects. This in turn creates roadblocks toward getting natural gas from places like nearby Pennsylvania, the second-highest natural-gas-producing state. Natural gas is responsible for more than 40% of all electricity in the U.S., and the U.S. is the world's largest producer of natural gas. It should be a no-brainer.

然而近年来，马萨诸塞州实施了相关政策，旨在到2050年实现碳中和。这意味着减少天然气、石油和煤炭的使用，增加太阳能和风能项目。Yet in recent years, Massachusetts has implemented policies to become carbon neutral by 2050. That means less natural gas, oil and coal, and more solar and wind projects.

政界人士对关闭地区内的燃煤电厂表示欢迎。最令人印象深刻的或许是2022年，时任总统乔·拜登站在该州最后一座燃煤电厂——布雷顿角发电站前，谈到了气候“紧急状态”。Politicians have cheered the closure of regional coal plants, perhaps most memorably in 2022 when former President Joe Biden stood before the state's last coal-fired power plant, Brayton Point Power Station, to discuss the climate "emergency."

强行将可靠的能源来源从电网中剔除，并不能让冬天变得不那么严寒，也无法让房屋更暖和。这样做只会减少能源供应，导致价格上涨——马萨诸塞州的电价比全国平均水平高出40%至60%——或者迫使人们更加依赖外部能源供应。Forcibly removing reliable sources of energy from the grid doesn't make winter less harsh or homes warmer. It decreases supply, which leads to higher prices—Massachusetts' electricity prices are 40% to 60% higher than the national average—or forces an increased reliance on foreign supply.

政客们可以侧重气候保护或价格可负担性，但无法两者兼顾。遗憾的是，今年秋冬，马萨诸塞州的居民们在收到电费账单时，也将体会到这一教训。Politicians can be focused on climate or affordability, but they can't do both. Sadly, it's a lesson Massachusetts residents are about to learn when they open their electric bills this fall and winter.
