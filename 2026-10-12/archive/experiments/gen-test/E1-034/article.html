航空旅行被推至战争前沿Air Travel Thrust Into Front Lines of War

胡塞武装袭击机场后，各国政府提醒公民谨慎前往沙特阿拉伯Governments caution on flying to Saudi Arabia after Houthi airport strike

BY BENJAMIN KATZ 2026年10月12日 · 《华尔街日报》A7 版 Oct 12, 2026 · The Wall Street Journal, p.A7

商业航空业已被卷入全球日益升级的冲突前线，机场、飞机及空域都成了导弹与无人机攻击的蓄意目标，其规模之大为前所未有。Commercial air travel has been dragged into the front lines of escalating conflicts across the globe, with airports, planes and airspace becoming the deliberate target of missile and drone attacks on a scale not seen before.

自2月份美国和以色列发起对伊朗的战争以来，中东地区的主要机场屡遭袭击。停泊在机场的飞机遭到导弹或拦截系统产生的碎片击中，导致机组人员与乘客丧生。这一威胁在周六达到顶峰：胡塞武装向利雅得的哈利德国王国际机场发射导弹，造成至少12人死亡、300多人受伤。Since the U.S. and Israel started the Iran war in February, major airports across the Middle East have been repeatedly targeted. Parked aircraft have been hit by missiles or falling debris from interceptor systems, killing crew members and passengers. The threat culminated Saturday in a Houthi missile strike at Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport that killed at least 12 people and injured more than 300 others.

此次袭击是十多年来沙特阿拉伯遭遇的最致命的攻击，导致各大航空公司匆忙下令让大量飞机改变航线，并取消了1,000多个航班。当局报告称，周日该关闭的机场又遭到袭击：一枚弹体击中后引发4号航站楼起火，造成人员受伤。The strike, the deadliest attack on Saudi Arabia in more than a decade, left airlines scrambling to divert scores of aircraft and cancel more than 1,000 other flights. Authorities reported new attacks on the closed airport Sunday, saying a projectile caused a fire in Terminal 4 and led to injuries.

美国、英国和澳大利亚等国政府已敦促本国公民在袭击发生后避免前往利雅得机场。法国、加拿大和德国则向各航空公司发出警告，称飞越沙特阿拉伯全境或大部分地区已不再安全。Governments including the U.S., U.K. and Australia are telling citizens to avoid the Riyadh airport following the attack. France, Canada and Germany are warning airlines that flying over all or large parts of Saudi Arabia is no longer safe.

专家及安全倡导者指出，旅客不应将航空公司决定执飞某航线视为该航线安全的保证。“这一切都取决于各国如何管理本国的空域，以及航空公司自身如何进行风险评估，”航空安全专业机构Osprey Flight Solutions的首席情报官马特·博里说道。“航空公司指望相关国家已采取恰当措施来确保空域安全，”他补充道。Experts and safety advocates say travelers shouldn't take an airline's decision to fly as a guarantee of a route's safety. "All of this is up to the individual country managing its airspace, and the airline to conduct its own risk assessment," said Matt Borie, chief intelligence officer at aviation security specialist Osprey Flight Solutions. "They're counting on the country to have done the right things to keep the airspace safe," he said.

自2月下旬以来，伊朗炸毁了迪拜、卡塔尔、巴林、科威特及其他地区主要机场的燃料罐，并破坏了停泊的飞机、跑道以及空中交通管制设施。与此同时，以色列的空袭也击中了贝鲁特机场的目标；今年7月，沙特阿拉伯轰炸了也门的萨那机场，以阻止一架载有胡塞武装代表团的飞机着陆。Since late February, Iran has blown up fuel tanks and damaged parked aircraft, runways and air-traffic control infrastructure across major airports in Dubai, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait and elsewhere. Israeli strikes have meanwhile hit targets at Beirut Airport, while Saudi Arabia bombed Yemen's San'a Airport in July to prevent a plane carrying a Houthi delegation from landing.

民用飞机面临的威胁日益加剧，这引发了负责监管全球航空事务的联合国机构——国际民用航空组织的罕见谴责。该组织上月再次呼吁各成员国防止航空业被卷入持续的冲突之中。The increasing threat to civilian aircraft has prompted rare rebukes from the International Civil Aviation Organization, the United Nations body that governs global air travel, which last month reiterated a call for member states to prevent aviation from being dragged into ongoing hostilities.

此前被视为中东地区少数安全飞行通道之一的沙特阿拉伯空域如今频频发出警告，迫使航空公司缩减航班运营——这里原本是连接欧洲与亚洲仅有的两条主要航线之一。The warnings over Saudi Arabian skies, which had been regarded as one of the few safe passages through the Middle East, are pushing airlines to scale back flying in what had become one of only two primary corridors left linking Europe and Asia.

由于全球冲突频发，航空公司不得不多次调整飞行路线。如今，可用空域日益缩减，而航空燃油价格又约为去年同期的两倍，这给航空公司带来了巨大压力。Airlines have repeatedly had to alter flight paths to account for global conflicts, with the rapidly shrinking airspace proving painful at a time when jet fuel is trading at about twice the price it was this time last year.

这些变化始于2022年初：乌克兰关闭领空，随后莫斯科也禁止欧洲、美国及其他国家的航空公司使用其领空。今年伊朗战争爆发时，途经伊拉克的重要航线被切断，促使更多航空公司选择经由沙特领空飞行。如今，越来越多的航空公司依靠一条途经格鲁吉亚、阿塞拜疆和土库曼斯坦的狭窄空域来实现欧洲与亚洲之间的航行。The shifts began in earnest with the closure of Ukrainian skies in early 2022 and Moscow's subsequent ban on European, U.S. and other carriers using its skies. The closure of a critical corridor over Iraq at the start of the Iran war this year pushed more airlines to fly through Saudi airspace. Increasingly, airlines have been relying on a sliver of airspace passing over Georgia, Azerbaijan and Turkmenistan to travel between Europe and Asia.

例如，自9月下旬起，维珍大西洋航空便开通了每日两班从孟买经中亚飞往伦敦的航线。此前，该航空公司通常选择经埃及和沙特阿拉伯来执飞此航线。Virgin Atlantic, for example, has been flying its twice-daily route from Mumbai to London via central Asia since late September. The airline previously routed it frequently through Egypt and Saudi Arabia.

航空公司往往别无选择。印度航空和土耳其航空已调整了部分航线，让飞机沿沙特与伊拉克边境飞行，以远离胡塞武装的攻击威胁。不过这些航线通常会经过叙利亚首都大马士革附近，而那里仍被视为危险区域。Carriers often are left with no good options. Air India and Turkish Airlines have shifted some routes so that their aircraft fly along the Saudi-Iraq border further away from threats of Houthi attacks. But those flight paths typically pass near Syria's capital, Damascus, which is still regarded as a risky zone.
