Authorities reported new attacks on the shuttered airport Sunday, saying a projectile caused a fire in Terminal 4 and led to injuries.

But the militants' escalating attacks, which they say are retaliation for Saudi actions against them, have hit airports and energy facilities—including the kingdom's main east-west pipeline and refineries—showing the growing threat along the southern border.

Saudi Arabia is a major source of funding for the global tech industry and artificial-intelligence projects. It also provides basing and overflight rights to the U.S. military.

对利雅得的袭击——该市距离也门胡塞武装控制区约 500 英里——已将沙特首都的部分地区变成了战场，居民们在爆炸声中四处躲避。周四，一枚被拦截的弹道导弹的碎片击中了一所幼儿园和一座医疗诊所综合楼。该市金融区的工人、一些国际学校的学生和外交官被要求撤离或寻找避难所。Trump told reporters over the weekend that the U.S. was in communication with the Houthis but might join Saudi strikes on the militant group.

在过去十年中，这位王储投入了大量资金，用于一系列具有未来感的项目，这些项目具有极其复杂的工程要求，旨在展示沙特王国的现代化进程，并吸引投资和旅游。这些项目包括未来之城 Neom、位于利雅得的一座巨大的立方体建筑以及一座沙漠滑雪胜地。总成本高达数万亿美元——对年度预算仅约 3500 亿美元的政府而言可谓野心勃勃。He has few good options, said Kristian Coates Ulrichsen, a Persian Gulf expert and fellow with Rice University's Baker Institute. 与伊朗的战争所带来的成本和经济损失，使得原本就不太可能实现的目标更加难以实现。Saudi press officials didn't respond to a request for comment.

今年前两个季度，外国投资有所下降，并且远低于王储设定的 2030 年目标。不断上升的国防和基础设施成本可能会挤占用于推动经济多元化的资金。即使是沙特王国努力成为人工智能中心，现在也面临新的风险，数据中心正逐渐成为袭击目标。The crown prince has plowed funds over the past decade into a slate of futuristic projects with extraordinarily complex engineering requirements aimed at signaling the kingdom's modernization and drawing investment and tourism. They include the futuristic city of Neom, an enormous cube-shaped building in Riyadh and a desert ski resort. The total cost ran to the trillions of dollars—aggressive for a government with an annual budget of around $350 billion.

许多由王储推动的项目都已停滞或正在接受审查。The costs and economic damage of war with Iran are making that already unlikely math impossible to balance.

根据凯投宏观的分析，该国经济中非石油领域的规模在第二季度低于第一季度。沙特财政部现在预计，2026年国内生产总值将下降3.6%。Foreign investment fell in the first two quarters of the year and is running well behind the crown prince's 2030 target. Rising defense and infrastructure costs threaten to siphon away money needed for the economic diversification push. Even the kingdom's push to become an AI hub now carries new risks, with data centers emerging as targets.

今年年初，该国设法将石油通过管道输送到红海，以摆脱伊朗对霍尔木兹海峡的控制。但在7月，胡塞武装宣布对沙特阿拉伯实施海上封锁，使红海航运面临风险。9月，对东西管道的袭击直接威胁到该国从霍尔木兹海峡逃离的主要通道。Many of the projects pushed by the crown prince are stalled or under review.

2015年，王储使沙特阿拉伯卷入了也门内战，支持该国得到国际承认的政府，与胡塞武装作战。沙特阿拉伯发动了空袭，加剧了冲突，但也招致了胡塞武装对其石油设施和包括利雅得在内的城市发动的袭击。双方陷入僵局，并在2022年达成了停火协议。The non-oil side of the country's economy was smaller in the second quarter than in the first, according to Capital Economics. The Saudi Finance Ministry now expects GDP to shrink by 3.6% in 2026.

新一轮冲突爆发之际，王储正努力控制与伊朗的对抗。战争初期，德黑兰发动的袭击表明，海湾地区的能源设施和机场等经济资产面临风险。据了解沙特阿拉伯内部情况的人士称，沙特阿拉伯也在努力在广阔的国土上部署防空系统，并正在调动防空部队，以保护宫殿和重要的能源基础设施。这些人士表示，虽然王储支持继续对伊朗施加经济压力，但他也在指示他的顾问，要与伊朗保持外交沟通渠道畅通，并明确表示该国的意图并非对抗。The kingdom was able earlier in the year to pump oil across the desert to the Red Sea to escape Iran's chokehold on the Strait of Hormuz. But in July, the Houthis announced a maritime blockade of Saudi Arabia, putting Red Sea shipping at risk. In September, an attack on the East-West pipeline struck at the kingdom's main escape route from Hormuz.

这些人士称，该国已向其军事盟友巴基斯坦寻求援助，并正在全球范围内寻找防空系统。他们还表示，该国也在寻求招募外国战士，以帮助其在也门的盟友。The crown prince got Saudi Arabia involved in Yemen's civil war in 2015, backing the country's internationally recognized government in a fight against the Houthis. It carried out airstrikes and inflamed the conflict, but also invited Houthi attacks on its oil facilities and cities including Riyadh. The sides fought to a stalemate and a ceasefire in 2022.

The new outbreak came as the crown prince was working to contain the confrontation with Iran. Attacks by Tehran early in the war demonstrated the risk to energy facilities and economic assets like airports across the Gulf. Saudi Arabia was also struggling to cover the vast country with air defense and was shifting batteries around to try to protect palaces and key energy infrastructure, people familiar with the kingdom's deliberations said. While supportive of continued economic pressure on Iran, the crown prince was telling his advisers to keep the diplomatic channel open with Iran and make clear the kingdom's intentions weren't confrontational, the people said.

The kingdom has called for help from its military ally Pakistan and is scouring the globe for air defenses, the people said. It is also looking to recruit foreign fighters to help its Yemeni allies, they said.
