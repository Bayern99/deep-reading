对抗俄罗斯新型喷气式无人机的竞赛The Race to Defeat Russia’s Jet Drones

西方国家需要密切关注，因为乌克兰正在抵御一种新型俄罗斯武器。The West needs to pay attention as Ukraine defends itself against a novel Russian weapon

Jillian Kay Melchior, Kyiv, Ukraine 2026年10月12日 · 《华尔街日报》A17 版 Oct 12, 2026 · The Wall Street Journal, p.A17

现在，所有从事国防工作的人都知道全球“爱国者”和其他高端拦截器存在短缺。但在这里的战争表明，武器创新周期是持续不断的，廉价的无人机也可能成为一个同样严重的问题。如果技术或规模上落后于对手，你将付出血的代价。By now everybody in defense knows about the global shortage of Patriots and other high-end interceptors. But the war here is showing that the arms innovation cycle is relentless and cheap drones can become an equally dire problem. Fall behind in technology or scale to counter them, and you pay in blood.

俄罗斯最新的突破是喷气动力无人机，而乌克兰缺乏有效的应对手段。好消息是，基辅已经制定了一个快速追赶的框架。西方应该密切关注——西方自己可能也需要这样一套体系。Russia's newest breakthrough is a jet-powered drone, and Ukraine lacks a good response. The good news is that Kyiv has a framework for catching up quickly. The West should pay attention. It may need one.

俄罗斯的喷气动力无人机时速可达 375 英里，最大飞行高度约为 5.5 英里——对于乌克兰目前拥有的拦截无人机来说速度太快，对于便携式防空系统来说高度太高。喷气动力无人机的射程接近 600 英里，最大载荷接近 200 磅。乌克兰拦截的比例仅为 55%，而拦截较旧的“沙赫德”无人机的比例超过 90%。克里姆林宫每天发射多达 100 架喷气动力无人机。每架无人机的成本为 10 万美元或更低。提高产量是俄罗斯的优先事项。Russia's jet drones can fly up to 375 miles an hour with a maximum altitude of about 5.5 miles—too fast for Ukraine's current stock of interceptor drones and too high for man-portable air-defense systems. Jet drones have a range of nearly 600 miles and carry a maximum payload of almost 200 pounds. Ukraine intercepts only 55% of them, compared with more than 90% of the older Shaheds. The Kremlin is launching up to 100 jet drones a day. They cost $100,000 or less each. Ramping up production is a Russian priority.

不只是乌克兰的问题Not Just Ukraine’s Problem

喷气动力无人机不仅仅是乌克兰的问题。弗拉基米尔·普京想要瓦解北大西洋公约组织，并正在测试他在欧洲可以采取何种程度的侵略行为。在对乌克兰进行空中袭击的同时，越来越多的无人机进入了北约领空。欧洲多次未能探测到在敏感军事地点和基础设施上空盘旋的可疑无人机。位于伦敦的国际战略研究所警告说，俄罗斯很可能参与了这些入侵事件，并正在探测防空系统的漏洞。Jet drones aren't only a Ukrainian problem. Vladimir Putin wants to break the North Atlantic Treaty Organization and is testing what level of aggression he can get away with in Europe. An increasing number of drones are straying into NATO airspace amid aerial attacks on Ukraine. Europe has repeatedly failed to detect suspicious drones over sensitive military sites and infrastructure, and the London-based International Institute for Strategic Studies has warned that Russia is likely behind these incursions and probing for air-defense vulnerabilities.

丹麦国防情报部门最近警告说，存在“较低但日益增长的风险”，即对“一个或几个与俄罗斯接壤的北约国家”进行“有限的”军事袭击。西班牙《世界报》于 9 月底报道，美国情报部门警告说，莫斯科可能会从地中海的船只上向欧洲发动无人机袭击。莫斯科与中国、伊朗和朝鲜分享有关无人机技术和战术的信息。上周，由于担心伊朗的无人机袭击，美国将其 B-1 轰炸机从英国军事基地转移。Danish defense intelligence recently warned of “a low but growing risk” of a “limited” military attack “against one or several NATO countries bordering Russia.” The Spanish publication El Mundo reported in late September about warnings from U.S. intelligence that Moscow could launch a drone attack on Europe from ships in the Mediterranean. Moscow shares information about drone technologies and tactics with China, Iran and North Korea. Last week concerns about an Iranian drone attack prompted the U.S. to move its B-1 bombers from a British military base.

《世界报》的报道重点关注俄罗斯较小的“天竺葵”无人机，但较旧型号的“沙赫德”无人机也可以从船只甲板上发射，而且喷气动力无人机很可能也可以这样做。一位著名的俄罗斯军事博主最近发布了地图，其中标明了美国、英国、法国和波兰潜在的喷气动力无人机目标。目标包括主要的军事基地、科技和国防公司、核电站、港口和机场。威慑需要识别这些威胁的能力以及具有成本效益的应对措施，但对手有理由怀疑西方既缺乏识别威胁的能力，也缺乏有效的应对措施。The El Mundo report focused on Russia's smaller Gerbera drones, but older models of Shaheds can be launched from a ship deck, as can jet drones in all likelihood. A prominent Russian military blogger recently published maps with potential jet-drone targets in the U.S., the U.K., France and Poland. Targets include major military bases, tech and defense companies, nuclear power plants, ports and airports. Deterrence requires the ability to identify such threats and a cost-effective countermeasure, but adversaries have reason to suspect the West lacks both.

乌克兰的应对Ukraine’s Response

为了了解乌克兰是如何应对的，上个月我参观了一个保护基辅的防空中心，这是全国几个此类中心之一。我采访了指挥官和副指挥官，但同意不公开他们的姓名，因为他们的工作非常敏感。To understand how Ukraine is responding, last month I visited an air-defense center protecting Kyiv, one of several such hubs nationwide. I interviewed the commander and deputy commander but agreed not to publish their names, given the sensitivity of their work.

乌克兰的防空系统收集来自前线和全国各地的数万个音频、视觉和热传感器以及雷达的数据。在防空中心，一张实时数字地图显示了乌克兰领空中的所有内容。该系统自动提供有关威胁数量和类型及其位置和速度的信息。总的来说，该系统可以提供多达 150 个关于空中威胁的详细信息，而未经辅助的人类只能识别不到五个。封闭式人工智能系统帮助乌克兰分析所有这些数据并采取行动，以进行及时的防空。防空中心还管理冲突协调，确保针对俄罗斯无人机的动能和电子对抗措施不会导致友军误伤。Ukraine's air-defense system collects data along the front line and across the country from tens of thousands of audio, visual and thermal sensors and radars. In the air-defense center, a real-time digital map displays everything in Ukrainian airspace. The system automatically provides information about the number and types of threats and their location and speed. In total the system can provide up to 150 discrete details about an aerial threat, while an unassisted human can pick up fewer than five. Closed-system artificial intelligence helps Ukraine analyze and act on all this data to mount a timely air defense. The air-defense center also manages deconfliction, ensuring that kinetic and electronic countermeasures against Russian drones don't result in friendly-fire damage.

俄罗斯的大规模无人机和导弹联合袭击旨在“压垮你们的防御体系，而应对之道之一就是把你们的反应切成小块”，一位副指挥官说道。电子战是目前最经济有效的反制措施。该系统能够识别敌方无人机是否容易受到电子战的影响，并据此采取相应的防御措施。如果无法奏效，防空中心将从地理和动能两个方面协调各级部队的应对，就像足球比赛中的区域防守一样。目标是在最早的时间点使用最经济的反制措施来消除威胁，所有决策都以数据为依据。Russia's massive combined drone and missile attacks are “designed to overwhelm your defenses, and one way to deal with it is slicing your response,” the deputy commander says. Electronic warfare is the cheapest countermeasure available. The system identifies whether an enemy drone may be vulnerable to electronic warfare and executes a defense accordingly. Failing that, the air-defense centers coordinate responses up the echelon both geographically and kinetically, like a zone defense in football. The goal is to use the cheapest countermeasure at the earliest point to neutralize the threat, with all decisions driven by data.

这种防空系统为乌克兰提供了先机，使其能够开发出一种廉价、可靠的拦截无人机的武器。基辅已经收集了大量关于新型无人机工作原理的情报，因此武器开发者知道需要解决哪些具体问题。目前有几种拦截武器正在测试中，开发者可以获得近乎即时的反馈，并据此进行调整。乌克兰的防空系统考虑到了快速的创新周期，因此任何新的武器都可以随时投入使用：不同类型的拦截武器，但采用相同的基本防空理念。This air-defense system gives Ukraine a head start as it develops a cheap, reliable interceptor for jet drones. Kyiv has collected extensive intelligence about how the new drones function, so weapons developers know what specific problems they need to solve. Several interceptors are in testing, and developers receive near-instantaneous feedback and adjust accordingly. Ukraine's air-defense system accounts for the rapid innovation cycle, so any new tool can be plugged in and used as soon as it's ready: different type of interceptor, but same basic air-defense concept.

坏消息The Bad News

坏消息是，在乌克兰开发并大规模生产拦截无人机的武器之前，“我们麻烦大了”，乌克兰国防和战略工业部前顾问尤里·萨克说道。未来还有许多重大挑战。考虑到新型无人机的高速度，新型拦截武器需要采用复杂的科技，而这些科技可能难以采购。“由于持续的袭击，扩大生产规模非常困难”，萨克先生说道。The bad news is that until Ukraine develops and scales a jet-drone interceptor, “we are in deep s—,” says Yuriy Sak, a former adviser to Ukraine's Defense and Strategic Industries ministries. There are major challenges ahead. Given the jet drones' high speeds, the new interceptors will require sophisticated technologies that may be hard to procure. “Scaling up is tough because of constant attacks,” Mr. Sak says.

在基辅，空袭如此猛烈，当局正在考虑把首都列为前线城市。这种威胁远远超出了对民众士气的打击。俄罗斯可以同时袭击乌克兰的武器制造商和能源网络。乌克兰的武器开发者需要可靠的电力来大规模生产，而对能源基础设施、港口和粮食运输船只的袭击也将阻碍乌克兰的经济发展，损害税收收入。基辅的最佳选择可能是与伙伴国家合作进行联合生产。Here in Kyiv, aerial attacks have been so intense that authorities are contemplating designating the capital a front-line city. The threat extends far beyond civilian morale. Russians can target Ukraine's weapons manufacturers and its energy grid at the same time. Ukraine's weapons developers need reliable power to produce at scale, and the attacks on energy infrastructure, ports and grain-export vessels will also hinder the Ukrainian economy, hurting tax revenue. Kyiv's best option may be to pursue joint production in partner countries.

照片记录的正是文中所说的威胁：喷气式无人机对基辅市区的直接打击。The photo documents the threat described: a jet-drone strike on Kyiv itself.

在此期间，乌克兰的防空部队正在利用其掌握的关于来袭空中威胁的精确信息，来减轻对企业和民众造成的危险和破坏。更精准的空袭警报可以减少需要关闭的企业数量，并避免在没有迫在眉睫的威胁时，将民众送往避难所。During this window, Ukraine's air defenders are taking advantage of the precision of their information about incoming aerial threats to blunt the danger and disruption to businesses and civilians. Better-tailored air alerts can limit the number of businesses that have to shut down and avoid sending civilians to shelters unless a threat is imminent.

西方能做什么What the West Can Do

西方可以采取更多措施，帮助乌克兰度过这个危险的时期。F-16战斗机是对抗新型无人机最可靠的防御武器，但乌克兰的使用频率远超西方预期的水平，而且现在太多战机正等着在比利时接受维护，无法出动。The West can do more to help Ukraine get through this dangerous interval. F-16s are the most reliable defense against jet drones, but Ukraine is flying them far more than the West anticipated, and too many are now out of commission pending maintenance in Belgium.

乌克兰未能为应对新型无人机的威胁做好准备，部分原因是由于财政限制，这迫使其在竞争性的国防研发和采购优先事项之间做出选择。欧洲已经冻结了数十亿美元的俄罗斯资产，它可以将部分资金转移给乌克兰，从而加强自身的威慑力。Ukraine was caught unprepared for the jet-drone threat in part because of financial constraints that force it to choose between competing defense research-and-development and procurement priorities. Europe has frozen billions in Russian assets, and it can strengthen its own deterrence by transferring some of that money to Ukraine.

吉莉安·凯·梅尔基奥是一位居住在伦敦的《华尔街日报》编辑委员会成员。Ms. Melchior is a London-based member of the Journal's editorial board.
