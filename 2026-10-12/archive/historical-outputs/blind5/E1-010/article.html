为什么高债券收益率不会消退Why Higher Bond Yields Aren’t Going Away

THE OUTLOOK | By Matt Grossman and Anthony DeBarros 2026年10月12日 · 《华尔街日报》A2 版 Oct 12, 2026 · The Wall Street Journal, p.A2

一些经济冲击看起来就像道路上的颠簸——例如关税，企业会进行调整，或者与伊朗的战争导致油价暂时上涨。但越来越多的经济学家认为，美国更高的利率将决定未来十年的经济走向。Some economic shocks look like bumps in the road—tariffs, to which businesses adjust, or a war with Iran that raises oil prices temporarily. But economists increasingly believe that higher U.S. interest rates will define the rest of the decade.

《华尔街日报》最近对经济预测者的调查得出了这样的结论。接受调查的分析师认为，近期长期收益率飙升至二十五年来的高点，并非暂时的波动，而是会随着通货膨胀的降温而逐渐消失。相反，他们预计更高的长期收益率将持续下去。So concludes The Wall Street Journal's latest survey of forecasters. The surveyed analysts don't see the recent surge in long-term yields to quarter-century highs as a temporary blip that will fade as inflation cools. Instead, they expect higher long-term yields to be sustained.

美国国债设定了整个经济中借贷成本的基调。更高的收益率意味着更高的抵押贷款利率，以及企业借贷成本的增加。这也会增加联邦预算的负担，目前美国国债的利息支出已经超过了军事支出。Treasurys set the tempo for borrowing costs across the economy. Higher yields mean higher mortgage rates and more expensive borrowing for companies. They also increase the burden on the federal budget, where interest on the national debt now exceeds military spending.

在10月2日至7日进行的调查中，71位提交预测的分析师现在预计，基准10年期国债收益率将在2026年底达到5.1%，高于我们上次（7月）调查时的4.4%。并非所有受访者都回答了所有问题。The 71 analysts who submitted projections in the survey from Oct. 2-7 now expect the benchmark 10-year yield to end 2026 at 5.1%, up from 4.4% the last time we asked, in July. Not all respondents answered every question.

预计更高的收益率将持续下去。预测者认为，到2027年中期，10年期国债收益率将达到4.96%，到2027年底将达到4.78%，高于7月份预测的4.3%和4.26%。Higher yields are expected to persist. Forecasters see a 10-year yield of 4.96% halfway through 2027 and 4.78% at the end of 2027, up from 4.3% and 4.26% in July projections.

自夏季以来，经济增长、通货膨胀以及对美国财政状况的担忧等多重因素共同推动了国债收益率的上升。许多经济学家表示，这些因素迫使他们重新评估了对美国国债需求的假设，而当国债价格下跌时，其收益率就会上升。A mix of improving growth, inflation and concerns about the U.S. fiscal trajectory have pushed bond yields higher since the start of the summer. Many economists said the cocktail has forced them to re-evaluate assumptions about demand for Treasurys, whose yields rise when their prices fall.

咨询公司Decision Economics的总裁艾伦·西奈在接受采访时表示：“这标志着支撑固定收益市场和经济的基本面发生了重大变化。”"It has been a sea change in the fundamentals underlying the fixed-income market and the economy," Allen Sinai, president of consulting firm Decision Economics, said in an interview.

他表示，即使在美联储上个月加息之后，美联储在控制通货膨胀的斗争中也已经落后了。西奈表示，仅仅通过一次加息是无法控制通货膨胀的，美联储似乎也通过其9月份的预测承认了这一点。这些预测显示，美联储的短期利率目标——联邦基金利率——今年将超过4%，并保持到2027年。He said even after the Federal Reserve's interest-rate increase last month, the central bank has fallen behind in the battle to tame inflation. Sinai said it would take more than one hike to smother inflation, which the Fed appeared to acknowledge with its September projections. Those showed the Fed's short-term interest-rate target, the federal-funds rate, topping 4% this year and staying there through 2027.

西奈预测，10年期国债收益率明年可能会升至近6%，并在2020年代保持在5%以上。美国国债收益率受到投资者对美联储未来几年将如何设定利率的预期的强烈影响。Sinai forecasts the 10-year yield could rise to nearly 6% next year and remain above 5% through the 2020s. Yields on Treasury securities are heavily influenced by investors' expectations for how the Fed will set interest rates in coming years.

近期通货膨胀的大部分上升是由于油价上涨，经济学家预计油价将回落：他们预测，美国油价基准将以每桶88美元的价格结束今年，低于周五的91.85美元，并在7月份降至80美元以下。Much of the recent increase in inflation is because of higher oil prices, which economists expect to recede: They forecast the U.S. oil-price benchmark will end the year at $88 a barrel, compared with $91.85 on Friday, and slip below $80 by July.

因此，他们预计按消费者物价指数衡量的通货膨胀率在 12 月为 3.5%，明年 6 月为 2.6%，到 2027 年底为 2.3%。与三个月前相比，这一数字几乎没有变化。美联储关注的通胀指标——个人消费支出价格指数，预计到 2027 年底将降至 2.4%，低于今年底的 3.5%。As a result, they expect inflation as measured by the consumer-price index of 3.5% in December, 2.6% next June, and 2.3% at the end of 2027. That has changed little from three months ago. The inflation gauge that the Fed tracks, the personal-consumption expenditures price index, is forecast to cool to 2.4% by the end of 2027, from 3.5% at the end of this year.

总部位于芝加哥的北方信托银行首席经济学家卡尔·坦嫩鲍姆表示，除了通胀预期之外，还有其他因素推高了预期收益率。他说，如果伊朗战争缓和，通胀压力可能会减轻，这将有助于降低收益率。但他表示，投资者仍然对庞大且不断增长的债务负担感到担忧，同时，人工智能基础设施的建设可能会提高增长预期和对资本的需求。他预计到 2028 年，10 年期收益率将保持在 5.2% 左右，然后在 2029 年底降至 5%。Carl Tannenbaum, chief economist at Chicago-based Northern Trust, said factors beyond the inflation outlook have pushed up expected yields. Inflationary pressure from the Iran war could abate if the conflict winds down, which would help ease yields, all else equal, he said. But investors will remain nervous about the large and growing debt burden, while the AI infrastructure build-out could lift growth expectations and demand for capital, Tannenbaum said. He sees the 10-year yield around 5.2% through 2028, before falling to 5% by the end of 2029.

财政与增长影响Fiscal and Growth Implications编辑小标题

如果收益率达到这个水平，将给联邦预算带来压力。2 月，国会预算办公室预测，未来十年，10 年期美国国债收益率不会超过 4.3%。白宫行政管理和预算局更为乐观，预计本十年后期收益率将降至 3% 中段。Yields at that level would strain the federal budget. In February, the Congressional Budget Office projected the 10-year Treasury yield won't exceed 4.3% for the rest of the decade. The White House Office of Management and Budget is even more optimistic, projecting yields falling into the mid-3% range later this decade.

经济学家们预测，今年的预期增长率将更高。他们预计 2026 年的 GDP 将增长 2.4%，高于 7 月份的 2.1%。这反映了对第二季度增长的向上修正，以及初步数据表明，经济在第三季度可能实现了超过 3%的年化增长率。受访者认为，此后增长将回落，未来十年剩余时间的增长率将为 2.1% 或 2.2%。Economists penciled in higher expected growth this year. They expect GDP to increase by 2.4% in 2026, up from their 2.1% July projection. That reflects upward revisions to second-quarter growth and interim data suggesting the economy might have grown by an annualized rate of more than 3% in the third quarter. Respondents see growth slipping back thereafter, to 2.1% or 2.2% for the rest of the decade.

同样，预测者们也不认为劳动力市场会出现重大变化。他们平均认为，失业率将在 2029 年之前保持在 4.2% 左右，与 9 月份的 4.2% 失业率几乎没有变化。Likewise, forecasters don't anticipate much drama in the labor market. On average, they think unemployment will hang around 4.2% through 2029, little changed from the 4.2% unemployment rate in September.

他们将未来 12 个月内经济衰退的可能性定为 24%，与 7 月份的 25% 几乎没有变化。They put the probability of recession in the coming 12 months at 24%, barely budged from 25% in July.

反方观点：劳动力市场脆弱性Counterpoint: Labor Market Fragility编辑小标题

并非所有预测者都认为收益率将保持在高位。佐治亚州立大学的经济学家拉吉夫·达万认为，劳动力市场比表面上看起来更加脆弱。Not all forecasters agree yields will stay high. Rajeev Dhawan, an economist at Georgia State University, thinks the labor market is more fragile than it appears.

他说，在过去六个月里，经济平均每月新增了 66,000 个就业岗位，但其中很少是在最具盈利性的领域。Over the past six months, the economy has added a solid 66,000 jobs a month on average, but few have been in the most lucrative fields, he said.

达万认为，疲软的劳动力市场可能会迫使美联储再次降低利率，从而降低收益率。“劳动力市场正面临着白领就业的困境，”达万说。“一旦美联储意识到这一点，他们就会转向降息。”In Dhawan's view, a weaker labor market could force the Fed to bring rates lower again, which could pull down yields. "The labor market's got a white-collar job malaise," Dhawan said. "Once the Fed realizes it isn't as strong, they will pivot to rate cuts."
