战争中，监控摄像头遭到黑客入侵Video Cameras Are Hacked for War

交战方劫持易受攻击的设备，追踪、监视并攻击敌人Combatants hijack vulnerable devices to track, surveil and attack the enemy

Daniel Michaels, Anat Peled and Milàn Czerny 2026年10月12日 · 《华尔街日报》A1 版 Oct 12, 2026 · The Wall Street Journal, p.A1

今年年初，美国和以色列军队轰炸伊朗时，以色列北部的一位养鸡户决定去查看他的鸡。当他在手机上打开摄像头应用程序，查看鸡舍外面的摄像头时，他惊恐地发现摄像头的语言已切换为阿拉伯语。As U.S. and Israeli forces bombed Iran early this year, a poultry farmer in northern Israel decided to check on his chickens. When he launched the app on his phone for the webcam outside his coop, he was alarmed to see that its language had been switched to Arabic.

他联系了以色列国家网络局，该局发现黑客已经控制了这款中国制造的摄像头。He contacted Israel's National Cyber Directorate, which found that hackers had taken control of the Chinese-made camera.

黑客对鸡并不感兴趣。摄像头的位置使其能够观察到以色列安全部队在靠近黎巴嫩边境的活动。They weren't interested in chickens. The camera's orientation on the coop offered it a line of sight to the movements of Israeli security forces along the nearby border with Lebanon.

网络局表示，他们帮助这位养鸡户加强了摄像头的安全性，并调整了其拍摄角度，以防止其继续拍摄军事行动。The cyber directorate said it helped the farmer harden

根据网络安全咨询公司S-RM的说法，这位养鸡户的摄像头是全球约12亿个连接到互联网的摄像头之一。[Continued on page A10.]

这些设备已成为日常生活中常见的设备，形式包括安全系统、门铃、交通控制系统，甚至婴儿监视器——网络安全专家和黑客表示，它们中的任何一个都可能存在被窥探的风险。the camera's security and adjust its viewing angle to stop capturing military operations.

威胁情报咨询公司Recorded Future的国家安全专家亚历山大·莱斯利表示：“我们现在看到，民用监控正在转化为战时情报收集。”他认为，无处不在的数字摄像头构成了全球性的“软肋”。The farmer's webcam was one of an estimated 1.2 billion internet-connected cameras worldwide, according to cybersecurity consultants S-RM.

我们现在看到，民用监控正在转化为战时情报收集。We are now seeing the conversion of civilian surveillance into wartime intelligence collection.
Recorded Future 国家安全专家 亚历山大·莱斯利Alexander Leslie, Recorded Future

《华尔街日报》采访了情报机构、网络安全专家、军事官员和黑客，以了解这种侵入式监控的范围和影响。该报还审查了数十小时的录像和数百张图片，其中大部分由黑客公开发布，并独立验证了其真实性，方法是确定其地理位置，并检查是否存在任何数字篡改的迹象。The devices have become a background fixture of daily life in the form of security systems, doorbells, traffic controls and even baby monitors—and potentially any of them are vulnerable to snooping, say cybersecurity experts and hackers.

隐藏的宝藏“We are now seeing the conversion of civilian surveillance into wartime intelligence collection,” said Alexander Leslie, a national-security expert at threat-intelligence consultants Recorded Future, who sees a global “soft underbelly” in omnipresent digital eyeballs.

情报机构在互联网上搜索易受攻击的摄像头，并通过测试数百万个密码来自动入侵这些摄像头。现在，人工智能使入侵这些摄像头并大规模分析其数据变得更加容易，从而将原本旨在提高安全性的摄像头转变为供作战人员用于追踪、监视和攻击敌人的工具——通常，这些工具的能力甚至超过了人类情报分析师。The Wall Street Journal spoke with intelligence agencies, cybersecurity experts, military officials and hackers to scope out the extent and impact of the invasive surveillance. The Journal also reviewed dozens of hours of footage and hundreds of images, much of it made public by hackers, and independently verified their authenticity, by geolocating their locations and checking for any signs of digital manipulations.

算法会处理海量的视频资料，以寻找隐藏的宝藏和模式，供情报人员进行监控。Hidden gems

“人工智能改变了一切，”OneFist团队的负责人克里斯托弗·科特赖特说道，OneFist团队是一个国际黑客组织，为乌克兰在与俄罗斯的战争中提供支持。Intelligence agencies scour the internet for vulnerable cameras and robotically tap in to them by testing millions of passwords. Now, AI has made it even easier to hack into them and analyze their data at scale, transforming cameras that were designed to boost safety into tools for combatants to track, surveil and attack the enemy—often surpassing the abilities of human intelligence analysts.

俄罗斯和乌克兰都在战争中加大了对摄像头的入侵力度，旨在互相监视、寻找新的目标并评估其攻击造成的损害。以色列长期以来都是数字间谍技术的行家，它入侵了伊朗的交通摄像头，以帮助其军队收集情报，这些情报被用于今年早些时候对伊朗最高领袖阿里·哈梅内伊的暗杀行动。伊朗及其代理人也进行了反击，入侵了数百个以色列网络摄像头和安全摄像头，这些摄像头被以色列官员认为对国家安全至关重要，例如安装在鸡舍上的摄像头。Algorithms digest endless reams of footage to find hidden gems and patterns for agents to monitor.

公寓管理协会“AI changed everything,” said Kristopher Kortright, leader of Team OneFist, an international hacker group supporting Ukraine in its war with Russia.

“他们无法通过双筒望远镜从伊朗看到以色列，”以色列国家网络局的一位网络安全专家埃利亚夫·努里说道。“因此，他们利用暴露在互联网上的摄像头，并将它们变成观察哨。”Russia and Ukraine have ramped up camera-hacking in their war, aiming to spy on each other, find new targets and assess damage from their attacks. Israel, long a master of digital spycraft, hacked into Iranian traffic cameras to help forces collect intelligence used in the assassination of Iranian Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei earlier this year. Iran and its proxies have struck back by breaking into hundreds of Israeli webcams and security cameras that Israeli officials consider sensitive to national security, like the one on the chicken coop.

2024年，俄罗斯黑客控制了基辅的住宅网络摄像头，其中包括一个由公寓管理协会安装的摄像头，用于监控社区。Condo association

乌克兰安全局（SBU）表示，俄罗斯打算利用被入侵的视频流来寻找目标，并“调整对乌克兰的导弹袭击”，从而改进其后续打击的瞄准精度。SBU表示，自俄罗斯于2022年入侵以来，它已经识别并加固、重新定向或阻止了大约4万个可能构成安全风险的视频摄像头。“They can't look through binoculars” to see Israel from Iran, said Eliav Noori, a cybersecurity expert at Israel's cyber directorate. “So they take advantage of cameras that are exposed to the internet and turn them into lookout points.”

俄罗斯的行动范围超出了战争区域。荷兰情报机构在7月份警告说，“俄罗斯国家行为者正在系统地进行数字间谍行动”，通过入侵欧洲各地的摄像头来实现这一目标。这些行动旨在收集有关军事运输路线、向乌克兰运送武器以及乌克兰军事人员下落的数据，情报机构表示。In 2024, Russian hackers commandeered residential webcams in Kyiv, including one installed by a condominium association to monitor the neighborhood.

网络安全咨询公司 CloudSEK 最近发现了一台暴露的服务器，他们怀疑该服务器属于一个讲俄语的黑客，服务器中包含数百张乌克兰国防、政府、能源和通信设施的网络摄像头图像，以及各种日常生活的场景。CloudSEK 表示，其中许多图像来自乌克兰与北约盟友罗马尼亚接壤的地区。Ukraine's security service, the SBU, said that Russia intended to use the hacked video feeds to find targets and “to adjust missile attacks on Ukraine,” refining their aim for subsequent strikes. The SBU said it has identified and secured, reoriented or blocked roughly 40,000 video cameras that posed potential security risks since Russia's 2022 invasion.

与伊朗的战争Russia's efforts extend beyond the war zone. Dutch intelligence agencies warned in July that “Russian state actors are systematically conducting digital espionage operations” by hacking into cameras across Europe. The operations aim to collect data on military transport routes, arms deliveries to Ukraine and the whereabouts of Ukrainian military personnel, the agencies said.

网络摄像头在与伊朗的战争中也发挥着越来越重要的作用。以色列国家网络局表示，一个与伊朗有关的名为“摩西职员”的组织入侵了耶路撒冷的一个安全摄像头，以监控一名巴勒斯坦武装分子于 2022 年实施的巴士站爆炸事件，该事件造成两人死亡，另有 20 多人受伤。Cybersecurity consultants CloudSEK recently discovered an exposed server they suspect belongs to a Russian-speaking hacker that contained hundreds of webcam images of Ukrainian facilities involved in defense, government, energy and communications, plus assorted views of daily life. CloudSEK said many of the images came from locations near Ukraine's borders with NATO ally Romania.

今年，与伊朗有关的组织入侵了 250 多台敏感的以色列安全摄像头，这些事件对国家安全构成了直接威胁，数量超过了往年。被入侵的摄像头包括车牌识别器和位于战略地点以及士兵聚集地（包括靠近黎巴嫩边境的军事哨所）的安全摄像头。War with Iran

以色列国家网络局表示，自伊朗战争开始以来，以色列境内被入侵的摄像头总数要高得多，并且在数十万连接到互联网的摄像头中，有数万个存在漏洞，伊朗黑客可以利用这些漏洞，例如使用默认密码。Webcams are also playing an increasingly important role in the war with Iran. An Iran-linked group called Moses Staff hacked into a Jerusalem security camera to monitor the 2022 bus stop bombings by a Palestinian militant that killed two people and injured more than 20 others, Israel's cyber directorate said.

被入侵的摄像头正在取代之前由空中侦察或实地间谍提供的情报，从而在目标定位、损失评估和其他作战情报收集方面发挥作用。入侵一个位置良好的摄像头可以让分析人员更接近目标，并且比渗透最狡猾的特工的风险更小。This year, Iranian-linked groups have hacked into more than 250 sensitive Israeli security cameras in incidents that pose immediate danger to national security, according to Israel's cyber directorate, more than in previous years. Compromised cameras include license-plate readers and security cameras near strategic sites and places where soldiers gather, including military outposts near the border with Lebanon.

限制区域The total number of cameras breached in Israel since the start of the Iran war is far higher, the cyber directorate said, adding that out of hundreds of thousands of cameras connected to the internet there, tens of thousands have vulnerabilities that Iranian hackers could take advantage of like default passwords.

视频摄像头通常在限制区域内静默运行。乌克兰从无法到达的位置入侵俄罗斯的摄像头信号，帮助基辅打击了莫斯科的军队和基础设施。Hacked cameras are taking over roles in targeting, damage assessments and other combat intelligence-gathering that were previously supplied by aerial reconnaissance or on-the-ground spies. Penetrating a well-placed camera can get analysts closer to targets and carries less risk than infiltrating the canniest secret agent.

在乌克兰战争初期，OneFist 团队监视了俄罗斯刻赤海峡大桥（通往克里米亚）上的摄像头信号，提供了有助于完善攻击计划的图像——包括俄罗斯在大桥下方安装了运动探测器，以阻止水上攻击。Restricted areas

Recorded Future 的亚历山大·莱斯利表示，来自被入侵摄像头的“信息可以缩短决策周期，这正是现代军队所关心的”。Video cameras operate silently, often in restricted areas. Ukraine's penetration of Russian camera feeds from unreachable locations have helped Kyiv strike Moscow's troops and infrastructure.

派拓网络公司云安全服务高级副总裁邹旭表示，虽然个人和企业网络管理员越来越重视网络安全，但像摄像头这样的联网设备可能内置的保护措施较少，并且不会得到持续的监控、修复或更新。Early in the Ukraine war, Team OneFist watched feeds from cameras on Russia's Kerch Strait Bridge to Crimea, providing images that helped refine attack plans—including the fact that Russia had installed motion detectors underneath the bridge to thwart waterborne attacks.

他指出，公司和政府内部的信息安全专家通常并不知道这些联网摄像头，而这些摄像头通常由设施管理人员或其他非信息技术团队安装和操作。“这些团队对网络安全意识和知识非常有限。他们的工作就是安装摄像头、打开电源，然后离开。”Information from hacked cameras “can shorten decision cycles in exactly the way modern militaries care about,” said Recorded Future's Leslie.

信息战While private individuals and enterprise-network managers are increasingly attuned to cybersecurity, web-connected devices like cameras may have fewer built-in protections and not be consistently monitored, patched or updated, said Xu Zou, senior vice president for cloud security services at cybersecurity company Palo Alto Networks.

派拓网络公司去年发现，绝大多数安全性较低的联网设备是网络摄像头和数字录像机。该公司还发现，视频流和视频会议设备——在全球各地的办公楼中普遍存在——尤其容易受到攻击。这使得它们在信息战中变得有用，而不仅仅是在军事行动中，保卫民主基金会网络与技术创新中心的研究员阿里·本·阿姆表示。Information-security specialists inside companies and governments often aren't aware of connected cameras, which are frequently installed and operated by facilities managers or other non-IT teams. “Those teams have very little cybersecurity awareness and knowledge. Their job is to mount that camera, power it on, and walk away,” said Zou.

他指出，反以色列组织使用被入侵的摄像头拍摄的图像，制作宣传视频和内容。“对他们来说，这关乎于展示他们的情报优势。”Information warfare

与伊朗有关联的组织汉达拉通过炫耀自己能够看到和听到以色列人在家附近的情况，来嘲讽以色列人。“有什么想说吗？找到最近的交通摄像头，然后说出来，”汉达拉去年在其网站上写道，该网站后来已被关闭。Palo Alto found last year that the vast majority of weakly secured internet-connected devices are webcams and digital video recorders. The company also found that video-streaming and video-conferencing equipment—ubiquitous in office buildings around the globe—are particularly vulnerable. That has made them useful for information warfare, not just military operations, said Ari Ben Am, an adjunct fellow at the Center on Cyber and Technology Innovation at the Foundation for Defense of Democracies, a Washington think tank.

在俄罗斯，工业和贸易部官员在今年早些时候的一次视频会议中抱怨他们过度依赖中国无人机组件，这时一个意想不到的声音加入了会议。“各位同事，你们能听到我吗？”这个声音问道。会议负责人回答说：“能听到。”然后，这个入侵者提出一个看似技术性的问题，但很快就变得粗俗。“关掉它，”会议负责人对他的同事说，这段视频后来被乌克兰黑客发布到网上。Anti-Israeli groups, he said, use imagery from hacked cameras in propaganda videos and content. “For them it is about playing up their intelligence superiority,” he said.

“物联网”Iranian-linked group Handala taunts Israelis by boasting that it can see and hear them close to home. “Have a message? Find the nearest traffic camera and talk,” Handala wrote last year on one of its websites, which has since been taken down.

连接到网络的易受攻击的配件是黑客的主要目标之一，这扩大了技术人员所说的攻击范围。通常，摄像头本身并不是目标，而是作为更大规模的黑客攻击中的薄弱环节。S-RM去年报道称，勒索软件组织Akira利用一个未受保护的网络摄像头，绕过了某个组织的网络安全系统，并对其进行勒索。一旦黑客进入本地网络，他们通常可以探测存储的文件、电子邮件帐户和其他设备，包括摄像头。In Russia, officials from the Ministry of Industry and Trade were grousing on a videoconference call earlier this year about their reliance on Chinese drone components when an unexpected voice joined in. “Colleagues, can you hear me?” the voice asked. Yes, replied the meeting leader. The intruder then asked a seemingly technical question that quickly turned obscene. “Turn it off,” the meeting leader told his colleagues, in a video later posted online by Ukrainian hackers.

S-RM美洲地区网络安全负责人保罗·卡伦表示：“物联网是一个非常广阔的领域，因此无论你是否意识到，一切都是你攻击范围的延伸。”‘Internet of things’

Vulnerable accessories linked into a network are among hackers' prime targets, expanding what technicians call the attack surface. Often, cameras aren't the target, but serve as the weak link in a larger hacking attempt. Ransomware group Akira tapped an unprotected webcam to get around one organization's network-security systems and extort it, S-RM reported last year. Once hackers are inside a local network, they can usually probe stored files, email accounts and other devices, including cameras.

“The Internet of Things is such a wild area that everything—whether you realize it or not—is an extension of your attack surface,” said Paul Caron, S-RM head of cyber for the Americas.
