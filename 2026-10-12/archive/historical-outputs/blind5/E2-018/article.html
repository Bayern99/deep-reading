融合主义Fusionism

原则的联盟。书籍：《融合主义：自由、美德与美国右翼的未来》，作者：斯蒂芬妮·斯莱德（圣母大学出版社，304页，30美元）。An Alliance Of Principles. Book: “Fusionism: Liberty, Virtue, and the Future of the American Right” by Stephanie Slade (Notre Dame, 304 pages, $30)

Aurelian Craiutu (Bookshelf) 2026年10月12日 · 《华尔街日报》A15 版 Oct 12, 2026 · The Wall Street Journal, p.A15

如今，在政治右翼中，人们很少听到弗兰克·迈耶的名字。2020年代的年轻保守派可能对融合主义知之甚少，融合主义是迈耶提出的政治理念——简而言之，即自由市场、美德和传统可以相互协调。自1972年他去世以来，融合主义的黄金时代已经过去，取而代之的是一种右翼好战主义，这种主义对妥协缺乏耐心。The name of Frank Meyer is rarely heard on the political right today. Younger conservatives in the 2020s won't know much about fusionism, the political outlook Meyer formulated—briefly, the idea that free markets, virtue and tradition can cohere with one another. Since his death in 1972, fusionism's heyday, it has gone out of fashion, replaced by a form of right-wing bellicosity with little patience for compromise.

斯蒂芬妮·斯莱德的《融合主义：自由、美德与美国右翼的未来》探讨了融合主义的知识渊源，以及更广泛地说，美国保守主义的知识渊源。起点是传统主义者与自由主义者和古典自由主义者之间的重大辩论：传统主义者将自己视为秩序、道德、宗教和美德的捍卫者，而自由主义者和古典自由主义者则倡导自由市场和个人权利。随着时间的推移，双方都没有在美国保守主义中占据主导地位；取而代之的是两者的“融合”。斯莱德是《Reason》杂志的编辑，她为我们提供了一份关于曾经具有影响力的政治理念的精彩描述，而这种理念值得我们重新关注。Stephanie Slade's “Fusionism: Liberty, Virtue, and the Future of the American Right” explores the intellectual roots of fusionism and, by extension, American conservatism. The starting point is the great debate between traditionalists as defenders of order, morality, religion and virtue, and libertarians and classical liberals as advocates of the free market and individual rights. Over time, neither side achieved dominance within American conservatism; what emerged instead was a “fusion” of the two. Ms. Slade, an editor at Reason magazine, has given us an excellent account of a once-influential political outlook that deserves renewed attention.

融合主义在1960年首次得到了充分的表达，即沙龙声明，该声明由年轻保守派在小威廉·F·巴克利位于康涅狄格州沙龙的家中起草。他们认为，保守派需要一个坚实的哲学和形而上学基础，该基础建立在有限政府、自由市场和个人权利的原则之上。对他们来说，自由和美德是任何良好社会赖以存在的两大支柱。融合主义者认为，我们需要自由才能拥有美德，我们需要美德才能幸福和自由地生活。“斯莱德解释说，美国保守运动的任务是‘协调’社会保守主义与自由市场经济……因为没有自由和美德，人类的繁荣是不可能的。”Fusionism was given its first full expression in the Sharon Statement of 1960, drafted by young conservatives in the home of William F. Buckley Jr. in Sharon, Conn. They believed conservatives needed a solid philosophical and metaphysical foundation anchored in the principles of limited government, free markets and individual rights. For them, liberty and virtue were the two pillars on which any good society rests. We need freedom to be virtuous, the fusionists argued, and we require virtue to live happily and freely. “The task of the conservative movement in America,” Ms. Slade explains, “was to ‘harmonize’ social conservatism with free-market economics . . . because human flourishing is impossible without freedom as well as virtue.”

融合主义者是自由主义者，但他们的自由主义与进步主义者的自由主义不同。这一点可以从他们对国家作用的理解中看出。在融合主义者的观点中，不能把经济、道德、宗教或生活方式问题上的权威托付给国家。它的任务是让个人保持自由，以便他们可以自主地追求一种充满美德的生活。融合主义者是保守派，但他们的传统主义与那些反对现代世界价值观的极端保守派的愿景几乎没有共同之处。Fusionists were liberals, but their liberalism differed from that of the progressives. This is evident in their conception of the role of the state. In the fusionist view, the state can't be trusted with authority over economic, moral, religious or lifestyle questions. Its job is to keep individuals free so that they can pursue a life of virtue on their own. Fusionists were conservatives, but their traditionalism had little in common with the vision of ultraconservatives who opposed the values of the modern world.

斯莱德认为，融合主义不仅仅是各派系为了短期政治利益而结成的联盟。它提供了一套连贯的共同承诺，其渊源可以追溯到美国建国之初。拉塞尔·柯克和弗里德里希·哈耶克持有看似无法调和的观点，但他们都比人们通常认为的更紧密地遵循融合主义原则。米尔顿·弗里德曼也是如此，他长期以来都处于融合主义的轨道之中。Fusionism, Ms. Slade maintains, was not merely an alliance between factions for short-term political gain. It offered a coherent set of shared commitments with a pedigree that runs through the American founding. Russell Kirk and Friedrich Hayek held views that seem irreconcilable, but they both adhered more closely to fusionist principles than is commonly understood. The same goes for Milton Friedman, who remained in the fusionist orbit for a long time.

书中讨论的另一个可以被认为是融合主义的人物是罗纳德·里根，他将自由主义倾向与对建立在美德基础上的自由的真正欣赏结合起来。遗憾的是，里根的世界观在斯莱德称之为“异见右派”的群体中已经声名狼藉。她将书的一半篇幅用于分析当前右翼的政治格局，并解释了为什么里根的愿景与当今的焦土政治格格不入。Another arguably fusionist figure discussed in the book is Ronald Reagan, who combined libertarian leanings with genuine appreciation for freedom anchored in virtue. Sadly, Reagan's worldview has fallen into disrepute among what Ms. Slade calls the Dissident Right. She devotes half of the book to an analysis of the current political landscape on the right and explains why Reagan's vision is deeply at odds with today's scorched-earth politics.

半个世纪前，保守派倡导自由市场和道德传统主义。如今，右翼中的许多人已经放弃了这种综合。A half-century ago, conservatives championed free markets and moral traditionalism. Much of today's right has abandoned that synthesis.
斯蒂芬妮·斯莱德Stephanie Slade

引言：“半个世纪前，保守派倡导自由市场和道德传统主义。如今，右翼中的许多人已经放弃了这种综合。”Pull quote: “A half-century ago, conservatives championed free markets and moral traditionalism. Much of today's right has abandoned that synthesis.”

从里根到特朗普：右翼的分裂From Reagan to Trump: The Right's Split编辑小标题

共和党是如何从里根走向唐纳德·特朗普的？斯莱德认为，当今的右翼民粹主义植根于里根之后的时期，当时新保守派与旧保守派之间出现了分歧：新保守派致力于自由市场和全球合作，而旧保守派则拥抱经济民族主义、保护主义和反移民政策。后者回到了早期的“美国优先”孤立主义，并在政治辩论中引入了粗俗的元素，诋毁了反对者和自己阵营中的异见者。在斯莱德的评估中，旧保守运动的代表人物是“手持叉子的帕特”布坎南，他的好战和孤立主义世界观在MAGA运动中得到了充分的发展。“旧保守派不太关心限制政府的规模，”她写道，“而是更关心确保政府被用来推动正确的事业、帮助正确的人。”How did the Republican Party go from Reagan to Donald Trump? Today's right-wing populism, Ms. Slade asserts, has roots in the post-Reagan years when a split emerged between neoconservatives, committed to free markets and global cooperation, and paleoconservatives, who embraced economic nationalism, protectionism and anti-immigrant policies. The latter hearkened back to an earlier America First isolationism and introduced coarseness into political debates, vilifying adversaries and dissidents within their own camp. In Ms. Slade's assessment, the face of the paleocon movement was “Pitchfork Pat” Buchanan, whose militant and isolationist worldview reached its maturity in the MAGA movement. “The paleocons were concerned less with keeping the government small,” she writes, “than with ensuring it be used to promote the right causes and help the right people.”

六十年前，迈耶担心一种异端思想已经渗透到共和党的思想中，这种思想要求不惜一切代价赢得选举，并且不重视原则或政策。如果他今天还活着，他不会对他的朋友们感到满意。异见右派利用移民和经济困难引发的不满和地位怨恨。他们的领导人将反对者宣布为叛徒，并谴责妥协、渐进主义和温和主义是软弱的标志。斯莱德令人信服地表明，异见右派认为我们制衡和有限政府的体系是腐朽的，必须让位于一种“凯撒主义”的行政权力方法。自称是保守派的人放弃了自由市场政策，并拥抱了巨额赤字和政府支出，以惠及共和党选民；他们的目标不是缩小政府的规模或限制其范围，而是为了赤裸裸的党派目的来控制和扩大政府。Six decades ago, Meyer worried that a heresy had crept into Republican thinking, one that demanded election victories at all costs and that placed no importance on principle or policy. If he were alive today, he would not be pleased with his friends. The Dissident Right exploits grievance and status resentments triggered by immigration and economic hardship. Its leaders declare opponents traitors and denounce compromise, gradualism and moderation as signs of weakness. The Dissident Right, Ms. Slade persuasively shows, believes that our system of checks and balances and limited government is rotten and must give way to a “Caesarist” approach to executive power. Self-described conservatives jettison free-market policies and embrace big deficits and government expenditures for the benefit of Republican voting constituencies; their aim isn't to shrink the state or limit its purview but to control and expand it for nakedly partisan purposes.

融合主义的未来The Future of Fusionism编辑小标题

一种更新的融合主义能否缓解这些问题？没有人能确定。为自由-美德的综合注入活力，可能需要放弃融合主义的词汇。也许一种新的保守主义类型将会出现，它反映并回应现代生活的复杂性和紧张关系。Could an updated form of fusionism ameliorate some of these pathologies? Nobody knows for sure. Breathing life into the liberty-virtue synthesis might require relinquishing the vocabulary of fusionism. Perhaps a new type of conservatism will emerge that reflects and responds to the complexity and tensions of modern life.

斯蒂芬妮·斯莱德女士认为，我们每个人心中都蕴含着融合主义的内核。她可能是对的。在21世纪，要找到一个能够主导社会生活的单一、首要的利益或价值观，无论是自由、美德、平等还是效率，都是不可能的。在冷战时期，随着集体主义在生活的各个领域取得进展，个人主义作为一种指导原则变得合理。但我们生活在一个由社会、经济、文化、政治、宗教等多个领域构成的复杂世界中，每个领域都遵循自身的逻辑，无法简化为单一原则。我们需要的，是在各种相互竞争的价值观和原则之间取得平衡，这种平衡应该是灵活的、适度的，并且能够抵制教条主义。斯蒂芬妮·斯莱德女士对融合主义的历史研究，或许能帮助保守派在黑暗时代中掌握这种平衡的艺术。Ms. Slade believes that there is a kernel of fusionism in almost all of us. She may be right. It is impossible, in the 21st century, to identify a single overriding interest or value that ought alone to govern social life, whether freedom, virtue, equality or efficiency. During the Cold War, as collectivism made advances in all areas of life, individualism made sense as a lodestar. But we live in a multiplicity of realms—social, economic, cultural, political, religious—that follow their own logic and cannot be reduced to a single principle. What's needed is a balance between rival values and principles that is flexible, moderate and resistant to doctrinal purity. Ms. Slade's history of fusionism might help conservatives practice this art of balance in dark times.
