胡塞武装发动袭击，沙特王储重担加重

迪拜——沙特王储穆罕默德·本·萨勒曼以积极的姿态开始了新的一年。他刚刚访问了白宫，并受到了特朗普总统的热情欢迎。即使在与伊朗的战争爆发后，沙特阿拉伯的处境也相对较好：它可以通过一条绕过受阻的霍尔木兹海峡的管道出口石油。

但沙特与伊朗支持的也门胡塞武装分子在邻国也门日益激烈的冲突，现在给该国的安全以及王储雄心勃勃但已面临困境的改造该国依赖石油的经济的计划带来了新的挑战。

周六，一枚胡塞导弹击中了沙特首都的国际机场，造成至少 12 人死亡，数百人受伤，其中许多人伤势严重。这是伊朗战争相关冲突中，针对美国或其海湾盟友造成的单次伤亡最严重的袭击。

据知情官员透露，一名 28 岁的埃及空姐在手臂被炸断后失血过多而死亡，她是袭击事件中失去肢体的一些受害者之一。

据熟悉情况的人士称，两座航站楼遭到袭击，屋顶部分坍塌。Storyful（一家视频验证机构）验证的视频显示，乘客在机场疏散时，拖着行李箱，从地面上一道血迹旁经过。Storyful 与《华尔街日报》一样，都由道琼斯公司所有。当局报告称，周日，关闭的机场再次遭到袭击，称一枚弹药在 4 号航站楼引发火灾，并造成人员受伤。

就在今年夏天，沙特官员曾向记者表示，胡塞武装的情况已经得到控制。但武装分子日益激烈的袭击，他们声称这是对沙特采取的行动的回应，袭击了机场和能源设施——包括该国的主要东西部管道和炼油厂——表明南部边境的威胁正在增加。

这场冲突正在危及沙特将旅游业发展成为经济支柱的努力，包括一项计划于本月底举行的备受瞩目的国际投资会议，而该会议能否如期举行如今越来越成疑。

战争的成本已经扩大了预算赤字，并有可能导致经济萎缩。

王储如何应对这次考验，将对沙特王国以外的地区产生影响。沙特是全球科技产业和人工智能项目的重要资金来源，同时，它还为美国军队提供基地和空域通行权。

特朗普在周末告诉记者，美国正在与胡塞武装进行沟通，但可能会加入沙特对该武装组织的打击。

DUBAI—Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman started the year on a high note. He'd just visited the White House where he was embraced by President Trump. Even after the war with Iran kicked off, Saudi Arabia looked relatively well positioned: It could export oil through a pipeline that bypassed the blocked Strait of Hormuz.

But the Saudis' escalating fight with Iran-backed Houthi militants in neighboring Yemen now piles new challenges on the kingdom's security and the crown prince's ambitious but already struggling plans to transform the country's oil-dependent economy.

On Saturday, a Houthi missile slammed into the Saudi capital's international airport, killing at least 12 people and injuring hundreds more, many seriously. It was the single deadliest attack on the U.S. or its Gulf allies in the conflicts related to the Iran war.

A 28-year-old Egyptian flight attendant bled to death after her arm was blown off, one of several victims to lose limbs, officials briefed on the attack said.

Two terminals were struck and their roofs partially collapsed, people familiar with the damage said. Video verified by Storyful, which like The Wall Street Journal is owned by Dow Jones, showed passengers rolling suitcases past a trail of blood on the floor as the airport was evacuated. Authorities reported new attacks on the shuttered airport Sunday, saying a projectile caused a fire in Terminal 4 and led to injuries.

As recently as this summer, Saudi officials briefed journalists that the Houthi situation was under control. But the militants' escalating attacks, which they say are retaliation for Saudi actions against them, have hit airports and energy facilities—including the kingdom's main east-west pipeline and refineries—showing the growing threat along the southern border.

The conflict is jeopardizing Saudi efforts to build travel and tourism into a pillar of the economy, including with a marquee international investment conference scheduled for the end of the month that looks increasingly in doubt.

The costs of the war have already widened the budget deficit and threaten to push the economy into contraction.

How the crown prince handles this test will have consequences beyond the kingdom. Saudi Arabia is a major source of funding for the global tech industry and artificial-intelligence projects. It also provides basing and overflight rights to the U.S. military.

Trump told reporters over the weekend that the U.S. was in communication with the Houthis but might join Saudi strikes on the militant group.
