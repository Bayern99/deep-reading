美国充电公司今年的公共电动汽车充电桩安装数量，预计将与2025年持平，达到行业历史最高水平。充电公司高管表示，他们计划今年增加数千个新的充电桩，旨在为那些希望更轻松地进行长途旅行的现有电动汽车车主以及涌向二手电动汽车市场的买家提供服务——同时为电动汽车需求可能反弹的那一天做好基础设施准备。

Charging companies in the U.S. are on track to install about as many public EV plugs this year as in 2025, the industry's record high. By adding thousands of new plugs this year, charging executives say they aim to serve longtime EV owners looking for easier road trips as well as buyers flocking to used EVs—while prepping infrastructure for a day when EV demand might bounce back.

ChargePoint（充电桩公司）首席执行官里克·威尔默表示，在谈及行业低迷期的情绪时说：“我仍觉得大家都还在惊慌失措（往纸袋里喘气）。但增长是真实的。电动汽车用户希望世界上有更多的充电桩。”

"I still think people are breathing into paper bags," Rick Wilmer, chief executive of the charging company ChargePoint, said of the industry's mood amid the downturn. "But the growth is real. The EV-driving community wants more chargers in the world."

自特朗普总统取消旨在鼓励美国人放弃燃油汽车的税收优惠和燃油经济性法规以来，电动汽车行业的乐观情绪一直不足。汽车制造商退出了他们最初雄心勃勃的电动汽车发展目标，取消了现有车型和计划中的车型，并列出了数十亿美元的资产减值，以重新调整其投资并转向燃油汽车。

Optimism has been in short supply for the EV industry since President Trump axed tax credits and fuel-economy regulations aimed at moving Americans away from gasoline-powered cars. Automakers backed away from their most ambitious pro-EV goals, canceling models and planned ones, while listing multibillion-dollar write-downs as they retool their investments and pivot back to gas.

尽管如此，根据充电行业数据公司Paren的数据，到10月初，美国新增了近2000个充电站，拥有超过12000个充电桩。这一数字与去年大致持平，去年新增了3885个充电站，拥有19839个充电桩。数据显示，美国目前拥有超过8万个公共快速充电桩。

Even so, the U.S. by early October added nearly 2,000 charging stations with more than 12,000 plugs, according to charging-industry data firm Paren. That is roughly on pace with last year, which ended with 3,885 new charging stations added with 19,839 plugs. The U.S. now has more than 80,000 public fast-charging plugs, data shows.

Paren的联合创始人兼首席技术官比尔·费罗表示，充电服务提供商正在从过去的一些错误中吸取教训，这些错误常常让电动汽车车主感到沮丧。

More crucially, charging providers are learning from the mistakes of the past that often frustrated EV owners, said Bill Ferro, co-founder and chief technical officer of Paren.

新的充电站平均拥有更多的充电桩，以缩短等待时间。充电桩的功率正在提高，以便更快地“补充”电动汽车的电量。此外，该行业还在努力改善其选址策略：在餐馆和繁忙区域附近增加更多的充电站，而不是设置在远离洗手间或其他便利设施的偏远停车场。

New stations have more plugs on average to cut down on wait time. Chargers are becoming more powerful to "refuel" EVs more quickly. And the industry is working on its real-estate game: adding more stations near restaurants and busy areas, not in distant parking lots far from restrooms or other amenities.

Auto-industry executives frequently say they expect EV demand to eventually rise again in the U.S., especially as less expensive electric models come to market.

Autonomous vehicles, which are overwhelmingly electric, are expected to drive charging demand.

燃油成本正在蚕食达美航空的利润。该公司周五大幅下调了对全年的预期，反映了燃油成本增加了 60 亿美元。

Fuel is taking a chunk out of Delta Air Lines' profits. The carrier on Friday sharply lowered its outlook for the year, reflecting a $6 billion increase in fuel costs.

即便燃油价格上涨，达美航空首席执行官埃德·巴斯蒂安（达美航空CEO）表示，他的航空公司——美国盈利能力最强的航空公司——最能应对这种剧烈波动。航空公司减少了航线，并设法提高了机票价格和行李托运费。

But even with fuel prices on the rise, Delta Chief Executive Ed Bastian said his airline—the most profitable in the U.S.—is best-positioned to ride out the wild swings. Airlines cut routes and managed to charge higher fares and fees for checked bags.

到目前为止，旅行需求依然强劲。但一些航空公司今年可能会亏损——与达美航空不同。

So far, travel demand has been resilient. But some carriers are on track to lose money this year—unlike Delta.

《华尔街日报》与巴斯蒂安进行了对话，讨论了行业如何应对当前的挑战，人们对新型高端旅行服务的需求是否会持续增长，以及为什么达美航空坚持不采用星链（SpaceX卫星互联网）Wi-Fi。

The Wall Street Journal spoke with Bastian about how the industry is navigating the turbulence, the never-ending appetite for new premium-travel amenities, and why Delta is sticking to its decision to skip Starlink Wi-Fi.

问Q《华尔街日报》：上次我们谈话时，似乎大家对燃油价格下降的前景更加乐观。您现在如何看待这个问题？WSJ: Last time we spoke, there seemed to be a lot more optimism that fuel prices were coming down. How do you see things now?

巴斯蒂安：显然，情况并非如此。很难预测未来会发生什么。因此，我们现在专注于我们可以控制的事情。如果考虑到燃油价格上涨了 60%，但公司的盈利水平与一年前大致相同，这确实令人惊讶，而且与过去燃油价格大幅上涨时的情况截然不同。

BASTIAN: Well, obviously that didn't come to pass. It's real hard to predict what's going to happen. So what we're doing is focusing on things we can control. If you consider fuel prices were up 60%, and the company earned a level of profitability comparable to where we were a year ago, it's pretty stunning, and certainly very different than anything that you would have seen within the industry going back over a number of cycles of prior fuel spikes.

问Q《华尔街日报》：您是否注意到人们对更高票价的抵触情绪？是否有人开始放弃旅行？WSJ: Are you seeing any resistance to higher fares? Are there people who are kind of throwing in the towel?

巴斯蒂安：我不会说人们放弃了旅行，但经济状况较差的人群对价格上涨更为敏感，这就是为什么我们减少了在该市场的航班数量。假日预订情况良好。我们预计第四季度的收入将增长 <span class="num">20%</span>。

BASTIAN: I wouldn't say throwing in the towel, but those at the lower end of the economic curve are going to be the most sensitive, and that's why we reduced our supply offering in that market. Holiday bookings look strong. We're expecting our fourth-quarter revenues to be up <span class="num">20%</span>.

问Q《华尔街日报》：高端服务的需求是否存在上限？WSJ: Is there an upper limit to premium demand?

埃德·巴斯蒂安：我认为现在还谈不上需求饱和。但仅仅因为有人表示要提供高端服务，并不意味着他们就成了一家高端航空公司。我们已经在这方面努力了 15 年。高端服务指的是整体体验，而不仅仅是更换新的座椅或增加星链服务。我认为，就整体高端服务供给与需求之间的关系而言，我们可能还处于发展的早期或中期阶段。

BASTIAN: I don't think it's getting crowded. But just because someone says they're going premium, that does not make them a premium airline. We've been at it for 15 years. Premium is the overall experience, not just bringing in new seats or adding Starlink. I think we're probably still in the early-to-mid innings in terms of the overall premium supply versus demand.

问Q《华尔街日报》：您提到了星链。当埃隆·马斯克批评你们时，您会感到在意吗？WSJ: You mentioned Starlink. Does it get to you when Elon Musk is criticizing you?

BASTIAN: I don't read it. All kidding aside, we know what we're doing in the space, and we've been the pioneer in Wi-Fi. Starlink's a great product, and we know it well. We were involved in the early years of bringing it to aviation, and they had a different business model than we had. We're happy with where we sit.
