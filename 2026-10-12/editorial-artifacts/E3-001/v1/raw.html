Decart不仅开发可模拟现实世界3D环境的Lucy与Oasis世界模型，其底层软件更能帮助开发者在不同AI芯片间顺畅切换，直接赋能AWS自研芯片Trainium3。 Beyond building the Lucy and Oasis 3-D world models, Decart's software lets developers switch seamlessly across AI chips—bolstering AWS's custom Trainium3 silicon against Nvidia.
