《华尔街日报》对71位经济学家的最新调查显示，美债收益率飙升至二十五年高点并非暂时波动——庞大的财政赤字、韧性增长与人工智能资本需求正在重塑未来十年的借贷成本底线。 The Wall Street Journal’s latest survey of 71 forecasters reveals that quarter-century highs in long-term Treasury yields are no fleeting blip—persistent fiscal deficits, resilient GDP growth, and AI capital demand are resetting the floor for borrowing costs across the decade.
