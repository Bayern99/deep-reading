赫格塞斯因直播行刑队而被批评 Hegseth Gets Heat Over Livestream Firing Squad

五角大楼宣布将在12月3日以行刑队枪决2009年胡德堡枪击案凶手尼达尔·哈桑并全程直播。这一65年来首例美军现役军人死刑计划，在白宫内部、国会两党与天主教会间掀起轩然大波。 The Pentagon's plan to execute 2009 Fort Hood shooter Nidal Hasan by firing squad on Dec. 3—and livestream the event—marks the first military execution in 65 years, drawing sharp rebukes from lawmakers, Catholic bishops, and skepticism inside the White House.

美军死刑核准链条 Chain of Execution Authority

1. 军事法庭定罪与量刑： 1. Court-Martial Verdict: 哈桑在审判期间自行辩护且未作无罪抗辩，2013年由军官陪审团判处死刑，关押于堪萨斯州利文沃思堡。 Hasan acted as his own lawyer and offered no defense; sentenced to death in 2013 by a jury of fellow officers and held at Fort Leavenworth, Kan.

2. 国防部长建议与总统专属批准权： 2. SecDef Recommendation & Presidential Approval: 现役军人受军队自身法律体系管辖，仅总统有权批准死刑。特朗普于10月2日依赫格塞斯建议批准。 Servicemembers fall under the military's legal system; only a president can approve execution. Trump approved it Oct. 2 upon Hegseth's recommendation.

3. 地点与时间设定： 3. Site & Timing Selection: 赫格塞斯计划在胡德堡执行，并选在哈桑当年开枪的同一星期几与同一时间点。 Hegseth planned the execution at Fort Hood on the exact same day of the week and time of day that Hasan opened fire.
