五角大楼宣布将以行刑队方式处决2009年胡德堡枪击案凶手尼达尔·哈桑并全程直播。这一65年来首次处决美军现役军人的计划，在华盛顿、天主教会及共和党内部引发剧烈伦理与法规震荡。 The Pentagon's announcement that former Army officer Nidal Hasan will be executed by firing squad—and that the event will be livestreamed—marks the first military execution in 65 years and has ignited bipartisan and religious backlash.

II · MILITARY LAW

65年首例现役军人死刑与“二战心态” A 65-Year Precedent & the "World War II Mentality"

美军死刑历史坐标 HISTORICAL BENCHMARKS DPIC DATA

WWII (1940s): 美军上一次采用行刑队（Firing Squad）枪决军人的历史时期。 Last era in which the U.S. military carried out an execution by firing squad.

1961 (Fort Leavenworth): 陆军列兵约翰·贝内特（John Bennett）因强奸与谋杀未遂被处以绞刑，此后65年美军未再执行死刑。 U.S. Army Pvt. John Bennett hanged at Leavenworth; no U.S. military execution has taken place in the 65 years since.
