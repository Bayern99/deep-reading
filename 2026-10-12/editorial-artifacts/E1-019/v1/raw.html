10亿美元财富税 vs. “42号提案”修宪防线（房地产除外） $1 Billion Wealth Levy vs. Prop. 42 Constitutional Shield (Real-Estate Carve-Out)

选民将在11月3日同时表决两项针锋相对的提案。若两项提案双双过关，围绕州宪法修正案优先权的法律战将不可避免。 Voters face two competing measures on Nov. 3. If both pass, a constitutional collision in the courts looms immediately.

01 / THRESHOLD
10亿美元净资产起征线 $1 Billion Net-Worth Trigger

打破美国常规所得税框架，专门针对净资产达10亿美元或以上的加州居民一次性征收5%财富税。 Departs from conventional U.S. income taxes by applying a one-time 5% levy directly on net assets of $1 billion or more.

02 / CARVE-OUT
修宪禁令与“房地产除外” Constitutional Ban & Real Estate

“42号提案”通过修改加州宪法，禁止对金融资产或个人财产（房地产除外）征收新税，从而令富豪税失效。 Prop. 42 amends the state constitution to bar new taxes on financial assets or personal property, other than real estate.

03 / COLLISION
党内分裂与潜在司法战 Intra-Party Rift & Legal Clash

州长纽森担忧最大纳税人外逃而反对财富税，加州民主党则在8月投票支持征税，双方正面交锋。 Newsom warns top taxpayers could flee, while the California Democratic Party voted in August to endorse the wealth tax.
