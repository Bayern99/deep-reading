01 · UNOBSERVABLE R-STAR

无法直接观测的“中性利率” The Unobservable "Neutral" Rate

哈马克对中性利率的估算值位居委员会最高之列——而且她认为，如果自己的估算有误，那也是估得太低了。涌入的资金与创造的利润表明经济并未受到抑制。 Hammack's estimate of neutral is among the highest on the committee—and she warns that if she's wrong, her estimate is still too low. Capital inflows and profits show an unrestrained economy.

02 · BOND MARKET YIELDS

长债收益率：是补充而非替代 Treasury Yields: Complement, Not Substitute

自夏季以来长期国债收益率持续走高，推升了全社会借贷成本并使金融状况更接近中性。但哈马克断然否决“债市可替美联储代劳”的观点。 Long-term Treasury yields have marched higher since summer, tightening conditions closer to neutral. Yet Hammack flatly rejects the idea that the bond market can do the Fed's job.

03 · AI & DATA CENTERS

利率工具的边界：AI与石油 Limits of Rate Hikes: Oil & AI Spend

外部批评者指出加息无法产出更多石油，也难以立即放缓科技巨头用现金和发债融资的AI开支。哈马克亦坦承：加息不会显著抑制数据中心需求。 Critics note higher rates can't pump more oil or immediately slow cash- and bond-financed tech AI spending. Hammack concedes raising rates won't materially slow data-center demand.
