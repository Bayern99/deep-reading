非常规提速引发的大陪审团困境 How Rushing the Process Backfired

1
人员经验断层：难以招募并留住检察官，临时聘用人员中有人从未担任过联邦检察官，需接受大陪审团举证“快速培训”。 Inexperience Gap: Struggling to retain staff, DOJ hired temporary lawyers—some with zero federal prosecutor experience—requiring a grand jury crash course.
2
绕过常规问询：华盛顿官员要求加快进度，直接传唤证人上大陪审团作证，跳过事先的FBI问询环节。 Skipping FBI Interviews: Washington officials pushed to subpoena witnesses straight to the grand jury without standard pre-testimony FBI interviews.
3
触碰机密红线：检察官事后才意识到证人需向大陪审团讨论的内容属于机密信息，最终不得不撤回传票。 Classified Information Blindspot: Prosecutors later realized witnesses would need to discuss classified material with grand jurors, forcing them to withdraw the subpoenas.
