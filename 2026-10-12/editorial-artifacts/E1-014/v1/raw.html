委内瑞拉涉嫌行贿者被捕Venezuelan Bribe Suspect Arrested

—Associated Press 2026年10月12日 October 12, 2026

一名在美国被通缉的商人，他被指控向委内瑞拉高级官员行贿，以换取在委内瑞拉获得有利可图的政府合同，已被拘留。A businessman wanted in the U.S. for allegedly paying bribes to top Venezuelan officials in exchange for lucrative state contracts in Venezuela has been taken into custody.

阿尔瓦罗·普利多于2019年和2021年在迈阿密联邦法院被起诉，这是对他的长期商业伙伴阿莱克斯·萨博的调查的一部分。阿莱克斯·萨博曾是现已被罢免的总统尼古拉斯·马杜罗的盟友。Alvaro Pulido was indicted in Miami federal court in 2019 and again in 2021 as part of an investigation into his longtime business partner, Alex Saab, a onetime ally of ousted President Nicolás Maduro.
