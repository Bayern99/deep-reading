一场二十年来首次启动的选民名册清理行动，据活动人士称导致约1.3亿人从初步或最终名单中被剔除。当选民资格争议与青年就业焦虑交汇，数千名安全部队在黎明前封锁了印度首都。 A voter-roll cleanup—the first in two decades—has cut some 130 million people from preliminary or final lists according to activists. As franchise grievances converge with youth unemployment anger, thousands of security forces locked down India's capital before dawn.

声称这是二十年来首次必要清理，旨在删除已故、重复或虚假选民，包括其所指称的从印度与孟加拉国东部边界进入的非法移民。 Says the cleanup—the first in 20 years—is needed to remove deceased, duplicate, or fraudulent voters, including alleged illegal immigrants from the eastern border with Bangladesh.

活动人士称去年启动的清理已导致约1.3亿人从初步或最终名单中被删，并与年轻人求职受挫的不满情绪交织，对莫迪构成严峻挑战。 Activists say some 130 million people have been cut from preliminary or final rolls, melding with youth joblessness grievances to pose a stiff challenge to Modi.

“Cockroach Janta Party”青年运动 The "Cockroach Janta Party" Youth Movement

  • 起源背景（春季） Spring Origins 在一次大学入学考试丑闻之后于今年春季应运而生，凝聚了苦于寻找工作的印度年轻群体的不满。 Emerged in the spring following a university entrance exam scandal, channeling frustration among young Indians struggling to find work.
  • 创始人与核心人物 Founder & Leadership 由阿布希吉特·迪普克（Abhijeet Dipke）创立，该组织公开支持选民名单抗议者并加入周六新德里集会。 Founded by Abhijeet Dipke; threw its support behind voter-roll protesters and joined Saturday's New Delhi demonstrations.
