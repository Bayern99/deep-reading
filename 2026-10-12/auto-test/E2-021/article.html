特朗普对林赛·格雷厄姆的柴油侮辱Trump’s Diesel Insult to Lindsey Graham

Review & Outlook (lead editorial) 2026年10月12日 · 《华尔街日报》A16 版 Oct 12, 2026 · The Wall Street Journal, p.A16

世界怀念林赛·格雷厄姆，尤其是在现在，特朗普总统在对乌克兰和欧洲的战争中，更加坚定地站在弗拉基米尔·普京一边。这位已故的参议员或许能够说服总统避免犯下这一道德和战略错误。The world misses Lindsey Graham, and never more than now as President Trump sides more decidedly with Vladimir Putin in his war against Ukraine and Europe. The late Senator might have been able to persuade the President to avoid this moral and strategic mistake.

特朗普先生于周五晚些时候宣布，美国将分阶段购买最多 480 万吨（近 3600 万桶）俄罗斯柴油。其中包括“立即”供应 30 万吨，11 月再供应 50 万吨，以及“此后立即”供应 100 万吨。Mr. Trump announced late Friday that the U.S. will buy up to 4.8 million tons (nearly 36 million barrels) of Russian diesel fuel in stages. That includes an “immediate” supply of 300,000 tons, another 500,000 in November, and one million “immediately thereafter.”

特朗普先生在真相社交上写道，另外 300 万吨的供应“取决于他们的柴油炼油厂的状况”。美国财政部在 4 月 7 日之前取消了对俄罗斯的部分制裁，以便其出口柴油。The delivery of another three million tons is “based on the condition of their Diesel Refineries,” Mr. Trump wrote on Truth Social. The U.S. Treasury lifted some sanctions on Russia through April 7 so it could export the diesel.

特朗普先生在真相社交上写道：“凭借我们对霍尔木兹海峡的完全控制，以及关于俄罗斯能源的这一重大消息，美国乃至全球的柴油价格将大幅下降，而且降幅之大、速度之快将创历史新高！”“Between our TOTAL CONTROL of the Strait of Hormuz, and this great announcement on Russian Energy, Diesel Prices for Americans and, indeed, the World, will be COMING DOWN, IN RECORD NUMBERS, AND FAST!” Mr. Trump wrote on Truth Social.

此举旨在帮助共和党在三个星期后举行的 11 月选举中获胜。但 3600 万桶俄罗斯柴油对价格的影响不会太大。据道琼斯能源旗下的 OPIS 称，2025 年全球每天消耗 2800 万桶柴油和其他馏分油。This is supposed to help Republicans in the November elections that are three weeks away. But 36 million barrels of Russian diesel won't move prices much. The world consumed 28 million barrels of diesel and other distillates daily in 2025, according to OPIS, which is part of Dow Jones Energy.

这项协议将直接为普京在乌克兰的血腥战争提供资金。而且情况可能更糟。《金融时报》周六报道称，如果基辅继续袭击俄罗斯炼油厂，特朗普政府“威胁要切断与基辅的重要情报共享”。What the deal will do is directly finance Mr. Putin's bloody war in Ukraine. And it may be worse. The Financial Times reported Saturday that the Trump Administration “threatened to cut off vital intelligence sharing” to Kyiv if it continues attacking Russian refineries.

9 月，特朗普先生指责乌克兰因其对俄罗斯炼油厂的远程打击导致柴油短缺。他完全无视伊朗和胡塞武装对中东能源生产和运输的袭击。俄罗斯向伊朗提供情报和武器。In September Mr. Trump blamed Ukraine for causing diesel-fuel shortages with its long-range strikes on Russian refineries. Never mind Iranian and Houthi strikes on Middle Eastern energy production and transport. Russia helps Iran with intelligence and weapons.

周日，特朗普先生宣布他所谓的“俄罗斯与乌克兰战争中的能源停火”。他说，“双方已经达成协议”。乌克兰总统弗拉基米尔·泽连斯基表示，如果俄罗斯也这样做，乌克兰支持这种停火，但截至我们撰写本文时，俄罗斯尚未对此发表评论。On Sunday Mr. Trump announced what he called “an ENERGY CEASEFIRE in the War between Russia and Ukraine. Both parties have agreed.” Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said Ukraine supports such a cease-fire if Russia does the same, but as we write this Russia hasn't commented.

一场能源停火将对双方都有帮助，因为乌克兰正面临又一个严酷的冬季，而俄罗斯的炼油厂正遭受围攻。但普京对乌克兰城市持续不断的袭击又该如何看待？An energy cease-fire would help both sides, as Ukraine faces another harsh winter and Russian refineries are under siege. But what about Mr. Putin's relentless attacks on Ukrainian cities?

他与普京达成的协议有助于克里姆林宫对乌克兰城市发动的战争。His deal with Putin helps the Kremlin's war on Ukraine's cities.

周三，在普里卢基市的一栋公寓楼上，俄罗斯的巡航导弹袭击造成22人死亡，其中包括5名儿童。第二天，一枚滑翔炸弹击中了克拉马托尔斯克市的两辆公共汽车，造成33人死亡。在宣布购买柴油之后，俄罗斯于周六向扎波罗热市的一个居民区发射了滑翔炸弹，造成至少20人死亡，其中包括3名儿童。如果不能实现完全停火，俄罗斯将继续对乌克兰平民进行迫害。On Wednesday 22 people, including five children, were killed in a Russian cruise missile strike on an apartment building in the city of Pryluky. The next day a glide bomb hit two buses in Kramatorsk, killing 33. After the diesel announcement, Russia launched glide bombs Saturday at a residential area of Zaporizhzhia, killing at least 20, including three children. Without a complete cease-fire, Russia will continue to torment Ukrainian civilians.

这里的战略转变很难理解。就在上个月，特朗普签署了林赛·格雷厄姆的法案，允许对购买俄罗斯石油和天然气的国家实施新的制裁和关税。现在，特朗普决定购买俄罗斯柴油，并取消一些现有的美国制裁。美国一直在向欧洲、印度和中国施压，要求他们不要购买俄罗斯石油，但所有这些外交努力似乎都付诸东流。The strategic turn here is hard to understand. Only last month Mr. Trump signed Lindsey Graham's bill to allow new sanctions and tariffs on countries that buy Russian oil and gas. Now Mr. Trump has decided to buy Russian diesel and lift some current U.S. sanctions. The U.S. has been browbeating Europe, India and China not to buy Russian oil, and so much for all that diplomatic effort.

克里姆林宫正面临来自西方制裁以及乌克兰打击俄罗斯境内目标能力的财政压力。能源与清洁空气研究中心估计，俄罗斯石油公司将从柴油交易中获得约45亿美元的收入。The Kremlin has been under financial pressure from Western sanctions and Ukraine's ability to hit targets in Russia. The Centre for Research on Energy and Clean Air estimates Russian oil companies will earn some $4.5 billion from the diesel deal.

特朗普还通过表示“现在是乌克兰更换总统的时候了”来加大对乌克兰的压力。但他必须四处寻找，才能找到任何支持为弗拉基米尔·普京提供资金的乌克兰人。Mr. Trump added to the pressure on Ukraine by saying it's “time for Ukraine to get a new president.” But he'll have to search far and wide for anyone in Ukraine who supports financing Vladimir Putin.

特朗普周末再次抱怨说，泽连斯基本可以多次结束这场战争。是的，基辅本可以随时投降。但乌克兰人更愿意为他们的独立和自由而战。特朗普渴望获得诺贝尔和平奖，他想促成俄罗斯和乌克兰之间的和平，但他唯一施加的压力都是针对乌克兰。Mr. Trump complained again on the weekend that Mr. Zelensky could have settled the war many times. Yes, Kyiv could always surrender. Ukrainians prefer to fight for their independence and freedom. Eyeing a Nobel peace prize, Mr. Trump wants to broker a Russia-Ukraine peace, but the only pressure he ever applies is on Ukraine.

他多次试图通过举行峰会、奉承、提供新的投资等方式来安抚普京，但这些努力并没有改变俄罗斯的立场。周末，一位俄罗斯官员表示，柴油交易不会改变普京实现其战争目标（即将乌克兰变成克里姆林宫的附庸）的决心。在这一点发生改变之前，特朗普的柴油交易只会帮助俄罗斯，而不会促进和平。His multiple attempts at appeasing Mr. Putin with summitry, flattery, offers of new investment, and more haven't moved the Russian. On the weekend a Russian official said the diesel deal doesn't change Mr. Putin's determination to achieve his war aim, which is to turn Ukraine into a Kremlin satrapy. Until that changes, Mr. Trump's diesel deal helps Russia and not the cause of peace.
