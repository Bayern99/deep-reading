股价表现：无线公司的艰难一日Share-price performance: Wireless Firms' Day of Woe

Share-price performance chart (intraday, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.): AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile US.

Shares in AT&T, Verizon Communications and T-Mobile US fell sharply Friday in the wake of SpaceX's $8 billion spectrum deal. AT&T and Verizon suffered their biggest one-day declines in more than 24 years. For T-Mobile, it was the worst percentage drop in more than 13 years. AT&T closed down 9.8%, Verizon fell 8.7% and T-Mobile declined 13%.
