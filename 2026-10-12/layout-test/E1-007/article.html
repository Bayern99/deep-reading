预测市场投注蔓延至陪审团裁决Prediction-Market Bets Spread to Jury Verdicts

BY MARIAH TIMMS 2026年10月12日 · 《华尔街日报》A1 版 Oct 12, 2026 · The Wall Street Journal, p.A1

在林赛·克兰西的夏季谋杀案审判期间，投注者对判决结果下了超过 10 万美元的赌注。由于陪审团僵持不下，案件于 9 月初被宣告审判无效，此后人们又下了超过 50 万美元的赌注，赌的是这位被指控杀害自己孩子的马萨诸塞州女性最终是否会被判有罪。During the summer murder trial of Lindsay Clancy, bettors wagered more than $100,000 on the verdict. Since a deadlocked jury forced a mistrial in early September, more than $500,000 in additional bets have been placed on whether the Massachusetts woman accused of killing her children will eventually be convicted.

在犹他州，泰勒·罗宾逊的审判日期尚未确定，他被指控去年在一次大学活动中杀害了保守派评论员查理·柯克。但在罗宾逊上个月正式提出无罪辩护之前，人们就已经下了超过 30 万美元的赌注，赌的是案件的结果。In Utah, a trial date hasn't been set yet for Tyler Robinson, who stands accused of killing conservative commentator Charlie Kirk at a university event last year. But even before Robinson formally entered a not guilty plea last month, more than $300,000 had been wagered on the outcome.

在这两种情况下，这些赌注都可以在 Polymarket 运营的一个境外平台上进行。该网站还提供关于联邦政府起诉肖恩·“迪迪”·库姆斯以及政府目前对被罢免的委内瑞拉总统尼古拉斯·马杜罗的案件的赌注。In both cases, the bets were available on an offshore platform run by Polymarket. The site also has hosted bets on the federal prosecution of Sean "Diddy" Combs and the government's current case against ousted Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro.

预测市场（prediction market）：对未来事件结果进行交易的平台。参与者买卖与"谁获胜""是否发生"等挂钩的合约，合约价格即群体对结果概率的集体判断。Polymarket 是其中最知名的境外平台。A prediction market lets people trade contracts tied to future events; prices reflect the crowd’s collective judgment of probabilities. Polymarket is one of the best-known offshore platforms.

当法庭变成赌场When the Court Becomes a Casino

预测市场引发了新的伦理问题和内幕交易风险，涉及从公司审计到美国军事行动的各个方面。这些问题现在已经摆到了法院门口，给司法系统带来了特别不寻常的挑战。Prediction markets are raising novel ethical concerns and insider-trading risks on everything from corporate auditing to U.S. military operations. Those headaches are now at the courthouse door, presenting particularly unusual challenges for the justice system.

“当公众将法庭视为赌场时，正当程序很容易受到腐败的影响，”全国刑事辩护律师协会主席 C·梅丽莎·欧文说道。"When the public treats a courtroom like a casino, due process is susceptible to corruption," said C. Melissa Owen, president of the National Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers.

最直接和最明显的问题是，陪审员可能会对自己的审议过程进行赌博，从而产生经济激励，促使他们达成特定的结果，无论案件的事实如何。The most direct and obvious concern is the potential for jurors to bet on their own deliberations, creating financial incentives to reach particular outcomes, no matter the facts of the case.

一些法院工作人员也可能掌握关于审议进展情况的内幕信息。Some court staffers are also potentially in positions to have inside knowledge of how deliberations are proceeding.

偏见如何渗入How Bias Creeps In

芝加哥大学法学教授 M·托德·亨德森表示，赌博平台的普及性和广泛性使得腐败陪审员的可能性成为一种真实的风险。The accessibility and pervasiveness of betting platforms makes the possibility of corrupting jurors a real risk, said University of Chicago law professor M. Todd Henderson.

他说：“但还有一个更广泛的问题，即大量赌注的预测市场可能会影响陪审员对案件的思考方式。如果人们下了数百万美元的赌注，认为被告将被判无罪，这是否会使陪审团更不愿判有罪？”"But there is a broader concern," he said, "that prediction markets with heavy betting could influence how jurors think about a case. If millions are wagered on the belief that a defendant will be acquitted, could that make a jury more reluctant to convict?"

“对于坐在家中的普通陪审员“Joe Q”（泛指）而言，”亨德森说，“问题是：如果他看到 Polymarket 上的赔率，如果他看到报纸或电视上有人谈论泰勒·罗宾逊是否有罪的可能性，他是否有可能产生偏见？”"For Joe Q Juror sitting at home," Henderson said, "the question is: Is he likely to be biased as a juror if he sees the odds on Polymarket and if he sees a pundit in the paper or on TV talking about what the chances are that Tyler Robinson did it?"

罗宾逊的律师向审理法官指出了 Polymarket 上的投注，并认为这种投注是“在道德上站不住脚的勾当”，把死刑诉讼变成了商品。Robinson's lawyers flagged the Polymarket bets to the presiding judge, arguing the wagering was a "morally dubious enterprise" that commodified death-penalty litigation.

辩方表示，在 7 月份的一次初步法庭听证会上，交易量非常活跃，并认为继续直播庭审程序会制造对罗宾逊不利的审前舆论。The defense said trading volume was active during a preliminary court hearing in July, and argued that continuing to broadcast the proceedings would create pretrial publicity that was unfair to Robinson.

托尼·格拉夫法官在上个月的一份裁决中写道：“法庭认可被告关于陪审团受污染及预测市场活动的担忧是真诚的。”但他允许继续直播庭审程序，并建议可以通过陪审团的选择来解决任何担忧，包括询问潜在的陪审员是否接触过预测市场。"The Court credits Defendant's concerns regarding jury contamination and prediction-market activity as genuine," Judge Tony Graf wrote in a ruling last month. But he allowed continued livestreaming of the proceedings and suggested that any concerns could be addressed in jury selection, including by asking potential jurors about any exposure they had to prediction markets.

离岸赌局The Offshore Books

通常，关于刑事案件的投注市场都是在离岸平台上进行的，因为预测市场公司希望避免违反美国的相关规定，这些规定禁止对敏感话题进行赌博，包括战争、恐怖主义和暗杀。Typically, markets for betting on criminal cases have been hosted on offshore platforms because prediction-market companies have wanted to avoid running afoul of U.S. rules that prohibit gambling on sensitive topics including war, terrorism and assassination.

尽管如此，市场观察人士表示，访问这些离岸平台并不困难。罗格斯大学的统计学教授哈里·克兰表示，大约有 20% 到 40% 的投注资金来自美国国内，用于在离岸 Polymarket 市场进行投注。克兰同时也是商品期货交易委员会的顾问委员会成员，该委员会负责监管预测市场。这项研究并未显示，这些资金是来自许多人进行小额投注，还是来自少数超级用户。Still, market observers say that accessing the offshore platforms isn't difficult. Rutgers University statistics professor Harry Crane said around 20% to 40% of the money bet on offshore Polymarket markets comes from inside the U.S. The study doesn't show whether that volume comes from many people making small bets or a small number of super-users, said Crane, who serves on an advisory committee to the Commodity Futures Trading Commission, which regulates prediction markets.

Polymarket 的发言人表示，人们“对现实世界事件的结果进行交易，包括法律、政治和经济发展。这些市场不会影响结果——它们反映了市场参与者集体认为会发生什么。”A Polymarket spokesperson said people "trade on outcomes of real-world events including legal, political and economic developments. These markets do not impact the outcomes—they reflect what market participants collectively believe will happen."

Polymarket 与《华尔街日报》的出版商道琼斯公司建立了数据合作关系。Polymarket has a data partnership with Dow Jones, the publisher of The Wall Street Journal.

法院的反制Courts Push Back

至少有几家法院开始关注这个问题。位于芝加哥的美国地区法院正在更新其内幕交易政策，以禁止员工利用工作中的信息进行投注。法院发言人肖恩·刘易斯表示，该地区的法官已经注意到了可能在陪审团审判中出现类似担忧的情况。“随着技术和社会的发展，法院将继续调整其政策，以确保公正和公平地进行司法审判。”At least a handful of courts are beginning to address the issue. The U.S. District Court in Chicago is updating its insider-trading policies to prohibit employees from using on-the-job information to make wagers. Judges in the district are on alert for similar concerns that might arise in jury trials, said Sean Lewis, a court spokesman. "As technology and society evolve, the court will continue to adapt its policies to safeguard the fair and impartial administration of justice," he said.

位于曼哈顿的联邦地方法院吸引了至少两个值得关注的投注市场。在去年 5 月至 7 月期间，Polymarket 的用户对肖恩·“迪迪”·库姆斯性交易案件的判决结果进行了近 79.8 万美元的投注。库姆斯被判犯有敲诈勒索罪和性交易罪不成立，但被判犯有较轻的罪行。The federal trial court in Manhattan has attracted at least two notable betting markets. Between May and July of last year, Polymarket users bet nearly $798,000 on the verdict of the Diddy sex trafficking trial. Combs was found not guilty of racketeering and sex trafficking offenses but convicted of less serious charges.

预计明年，马杜罗将在同一法庭接受审判，他面临的指控包括与毒品恐怖主义有关的罪名。Polymarket平台上关于马杜罗是否会被判有罪的预测，目前已吸引了约13.3万美元的交易量。另一个关于他是否会被判处监禁的预测，交易量已超过90万美元。Maduro is set to go on trial next year in the same court, on charges including narco-terrorism. A Polymarket offering on whether Maduro will be found guilty has attracted about $133,000 in trading volume so far. Another on whether he will serve prison time has seen more than $900,000 of activity.
